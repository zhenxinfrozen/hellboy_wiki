***Pancakes*** is set in 1947, when a young [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) eats pancakes for the first time.

## Publication History

*Pancakes* was published in black and white, as part of the *[Dark Horse Presents](/entry/Dark_Horse_Presents) Annual 1999*, in August 1999. The two page story was reprinted in color in *[Hellboy: vol. 4 The Right Hand of Doom](/entry/Trade_Collections)*.

## Synopsis

In 1947, a young [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) is called in for breakfast. He wants noodles, but is told by General [Norton Ricker](/entry/Norton_Ricker) he can only have pancakes. Hellboy does not want to eat pancakes, but tries one bite. Hellboy loves Pancakes. In [Pandemonium](/entry/Pandemonium_%28location%29), capital of [Hell](/entry/Hell), the Demons wail. Three demon lords, [Astaroth](/entry/Astaroth), [Mammon](/entry/Mammon) and [Haborym](/entry/Haborym), comment that now that Hellboy has eaten pancakes, he will never come back to hell.

## Story Chronology

This story takes place on May 12, 1947, according to the timeline in *[Hellboy: The Companion](/entry/Hellboy__The_Companion)*.

Hellboy asks for pancakes again in [*B.P.R.D.: 1947* \#4](/entry/1947), indicating that story takes place afte*r Pancakes*.

## Connections to Other Stories

In some ways this story is just a quick gag. However, this is the first appearance of the demon [Astaroth](/entry/Astaroth), who will make minor but significant appearances in *[Box Full of Evil](/entry/Box_Full_of_Evil)* and *[The Wild Hunt](/entry/The_Wild_Hunt)*. Further, the idea of Hellboy's refusal to serve hell is a continuing theme in the series.
