**Magic/Sorcery** is a supernatural, metaphysical force that can be used by various, if not all creatures including humans. It can create, transform and alter, even on fundamental levels, the world around us. There are not limitations to itself, save the limitations of an individual using it. It is neutral, but can be used for good or evil, wonder or destruction, like all things, especially the enchanting.

According to [Gustav Strobl](/entry/Gustav_Strobl), majority magic conjured by witches and occultists are made from pacts with demons. Magic was a power that mankind came to rely on after the loss of [vril](/entry/Vril). Several locations on Earth are filled with magic such as England. Hell was a fountain of magic that many Satanists and occultists drew power from. However with the death of [Satan](/entry/Satan) and the hierarchy in [Hell](/entry/Hell) in disarray the power has ceased to exist resulting many magic users on Earth to diminish in power.
