***Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus*** is a novel written by English author Mary Shelley (1797–1851) that tells the story of [Victor Frankenstein](/entry/Victor_Frankenstein), a young scientist who creates a life from corpses. In the Hellboy Universe, the story is considered a fictionalized account of true events that took place in the late 18th century.

For details on the book, see Wikipedia.
