**Ereshigal** (sometimes spelled "*Ereshkigal*") is an ancient Mesopotamian goddess of the Underworld, from whom almost nothing is known. She approached [Raimund Diestel](/entry/Raimund_Diestel), convincing him to become the first incarnation [The Black Flame](/entry/The_Black_Flame_(character)), while [Rasputin](/entry/Rasputin) described the [Ogdru Jahad](/entry/Ogdru_Jahad) as the "throne-bearers of Ereshigal ", implying that she controls the seven ancient dragons.

Her name was also invoked alongside Belial and [Lucifer](/entry/Satan) to summon [Amdusias](/entry/Amdusias) in "[Dark and Terrible](/entry/Dark_and_Terrible)".
