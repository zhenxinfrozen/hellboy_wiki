![The object's 1st appearance from "[Born Again](/entry/Born_Again)"](Spiked_Cylinder.jpg "The object's 1st appearance from "Born Again"") The **'Spiked Cylinder**' is an old artifact from [Hyperborea](/entry/Hyperborea) used by wise men to focus their power. It was found by [Roger](/entry/Roger) in the story "[Born Again](/entry/Born_Again)." [Elizabeth Sherman](/entry/Elizabeth_Sherman) used the object in *[The Black Flame](/entry/The_Black_Flame_%28story%29)* to focus her fire powers and defeat [Katha-Hem](/entry/Katha-Hem). The object was lost at that time.
