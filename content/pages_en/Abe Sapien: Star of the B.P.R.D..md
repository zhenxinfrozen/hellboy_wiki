***Abe Sapien: Star of the B.P.R.D.*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#4](/entry/Hellboy%3A_Weird_Tales)*. [John Arcudi](/entry/John_Arcudi) wrote the story and [Roger Langridge](/entry/Roger_Langridge) handeled the art duties.

## Plot Synopsis

[Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien)'s daydreams about being the star agent of the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) are interrupted by the braggadocios [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) showing up for work late and taking advantage of him.
