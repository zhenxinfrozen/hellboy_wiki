***Lost Ones*** is a short story featured in the *[Hellboy Winter Special (2018)](/entry/Hellboy_Winter_Special_(2018))* written and illustrated by Fábio Moon and Gabriel Bá. It is a continuation of the [*Vampire* story cycle](/entry/Vampire_(story_cycle)) of *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D._(series))*. The tale is set in Bulgaria, 1954.
