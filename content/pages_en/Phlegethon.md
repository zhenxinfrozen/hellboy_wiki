**Phlegethon** is one of the rivers of [Hell](/entry/Hell). Great Marquis of Hell [Shax](/entry/Shax) was killed by this river during the revolt of the slave demons.

It was later revealed that the embodiment of the river was with [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) when he arrived in Hell. After [Edward Grey](/entry/Edward_Grey) distracted [Eligos](/entry/Eligos) who was intent on killing Hellboy, Phlegethon took hellboy to and empty [Pandemonium](/entry/Pandemonium_(location)). Phlegethon then gifted the half-demon the dagger which he used to kill a sleeping [Satan](/entry/Satan).

Later after [Eligos](/entry/Eligos) was killed by [Edward Grey](/entry/Edward_Grey) who became the embodiment of the river [Acheron](/entry/Acheron_(river)), Phlegethon, along with [Cocytus](/entry/Cocytus), [Styx](/entry/Styx), and [Lethe](/entry/Lethe) welcomed their newly ascended brother inviting him to join them.
