**Anubis** is an Egyptian deity. According to mythology, he is god of the underworld, serving as a judge for dead souls.

## [World's Greatest Paranormal Investigator (SDCC)](/entry/World's_Greatest_Paranormal_Investigator_(SDCC))

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) encountered the god somewhere in the Midwest region of the USA, one in a string of towns that had had all their residents disappear. Upon arrival, he noticed a sign in the shape of a cowboy boot with an Egyptian scarab and a swastika on it. He also encountered a lonely dog, which grows to immense size, and Hellboy realizes that it is Anubis. Hellboy defeated Anubis when the god landed on a flammable gas pump.
