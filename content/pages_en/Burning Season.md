***Burning Season*** is a one-shot arc of *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.](/entry/Hellboy_and_the_B.P.R.D.): 1955* written by Mike Mignola and Chris Roberson, and illustrated by Paolo and Joe Rivera.

## Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) along with [Susan](/entry/Susan_Xiang) and [Professor Broom](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm) investigate a rash of spontaneous human combustions in a Florida town unlike any supernatural phenomena he’s seen before–and the fire has an appetite for [Bureau](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense) agents.

• Paolo Rivera returns!
