**Kevin Nowlan** is a comic book artist and designer. He designed the [*Hellboy* logo](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29) and has illustrated multiple stories.

## Hellboy Bibliography

### Interior Artist

Kevin Nowlan always colors and letters his interior pages.

- *[Hellboy: Buster Oakley Gets His Wish](/entry/Buster_Oakley_Gets_His_Wish)*
- *[Lobster Johnson: Satan Smells A Rat](/entry/Satan_Smells_a_Rat)*
- *[Abe Sapien: Ogopogo](/entry/Ogopogo)*
- *[Lobster Johnson: A Chain Forged in Life](/entry/A_Chain_Forged_in_Life)* (framing sequence)
