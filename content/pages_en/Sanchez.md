**Sanchez** is a fictional character from the *[Hellboy: Dogs of the Night](/entry/Hellboy__Dogs_of_the_Night)* game.

## History

### Dogs of the Night

He was once a monk in a monastery. He was mysteriously murdered, and [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)), having traveled back in time, found his body and severed head. Hellboy took his head and showed it in front of the door to a forbidden room in the monastery, thus fooling [the guard](/entry/Giant_Monkey) who, upon seeing the head through the crack of the door, assumed it was the real Sanchez and opened the door for Hellboy.
