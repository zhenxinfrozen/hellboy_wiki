***Plague of Frogs*** is the first cycle of *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D._%28series%29)* stories.

Following the release of *[King of Fear](/entry/King_of_Fear_%28story%29)* \#5 it was announced that future *B.P.R.D.* stories would come under the [*Hell on Earth*](/entry/BPRD%3F%E4%BA%BA%E9%97%B4%E5%9C%B0%E7%8B%B1)) title. All past B.P.R.D. titles come under the *Plague of Frogs* title, making it the first story cycle.

A number of *B.P.R.D.* stories published after this time were not collected in the [*B.P.R.D.: Plague of Frogs omnibus editions*](/entry/Omnibus_Editions). These stories were set prior to the *Plague of Frogs* story cycle and were collected in the trade paperback *[B.P.R.D.: Being Human](/entry/TPB)*.

## Collections

*Plague of Frogs* was originally collected in [trade paperbacks](/entry/TPB) without the cycle title, alongside other B.P.R.D. stories.

![](/content/images/BPRD_Plague_of_Frogs_Trade_Collections.webp)
670 px|center|The twelve <em>B.P.R.D.</em> trade paperbacks featuring stories from the <em>Plague of Frogs</em> cycle.

The *Plague of Frogs* cycle was later recollected in [omnibus editions](/entry/Omnibus_Editions). These feature a slight re-ordering of the stories for a better reading experience and expanded sketchbooks.
