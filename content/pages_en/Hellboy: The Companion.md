***Hellboy: The Companion*** is a 2008 guidebook to the *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(series))* series. The compendium is primarily written by Stephen Weiner, Jason Hall, and Victoria Blake, with additional material by series creator Mike Mignola and an introduction by [Scott Allie](/entry/Scott_Allie).

Much of the book consists of character profiles and an official timeline of the body of work. Artwork by Mignola, [Guy Davis](/entry/Guy_Davis), [Ryan Sook](/entry/Ryan_Sook), [Duncan Fegredo](/entry/Duncan_Fegredo), [Jason Shawn Alexander](/entry/Jason_Shawn_Alexander), and [Paul Azaceta](/entry/Paul_Azaceta) illustrates the book; this is largely repurposed from comic panels.

*The Companion* was written prior to the release of *[The Wild Hunt](/entry/The_Wild_Hunt).*
