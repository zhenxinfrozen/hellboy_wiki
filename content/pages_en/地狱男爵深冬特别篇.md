## Other seasonal one-shots

In addition to the *Hellboy Winter Special* anthology issues, there have been seasonal one-shots that have also been referred to in interviews as "winter specials", though they are not officially titled as such.

- *[Hellboy: Krampusnacht](/entry/Krampusnacht)* (December 2017)
- *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.: The Seven Wives Club](/entry/The_Seven_Wives_Club)* (November 2020, originally announced as December 2019)
