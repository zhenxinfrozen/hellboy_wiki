**Anna Walton** is a British actress who plays [Princess Nuala](/entry/Nuala) in *[Hellboy II: The Golden Army](/entry/Hellboy_II__The_Golden_Army)*.

## Notable Roles

- Other notable roles include Vicki in *Vampire Diary* (2007), and Severian in *Mutant Chronicles* (2008).

- She is now currently on the CW hot new show Reign and plays the king of France' mistress as Diane de Poitiers
