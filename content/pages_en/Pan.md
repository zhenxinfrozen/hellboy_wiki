**Pan** or the **Great God Pan** was a deity worshiped in Greece and revered by [satyrs](/entry/Satyr) and fauns.

## History

After centuries of years since [Ælita](/entry/%C3%86lita) and her village had been changed in half-man and half-goat creatures, Ælita's mother Sibyl had a vision that Pan would send the Ægipan to lift the curse and so sent her daughter to find the hero, leading her to locate [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)).
