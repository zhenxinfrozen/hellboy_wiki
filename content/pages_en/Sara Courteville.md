**Sara Courteville** is a fictional character from the *[Hellboy: Dogs of the Night](/entry/Hellboy__Dogs_of_the_Night)* game.

## History

### Dogs of the Night

According to the game book, she is one of the B.P.R.D.'s finest agents, and has proven herself to bе versatile, competent investigator and combatant. She accompanies [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) to the graveyard in search of [Peter Johnson](/entry/Peter_Johnson).

At some point, she is separated from Hellboy and kidnapped by [Kalin](/entry/Kalin), who sends her to Hell, but Hellboy eventually saves her.
