Part 2 of 3 of *[Hellboy Animated](/entry/Hellboy_Animated)* tie-in series.

## Synopsis

The story follows Hellboy and the B.P.R.D. as they investigate supernatural threats targeting children in a Polish village. The story connects to Professor Trevor Bruttenholm's (Dr. Broom's) history, with past events coming back to haunt the present. A mysterious antagonist from Broom's earlier experiences emerges to terrorize the village's children, forcing the team to confront both supernatural dangers and the consequences of past actions.
