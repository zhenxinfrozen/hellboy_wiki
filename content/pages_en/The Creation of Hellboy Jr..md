***The Creation of Hellboy Jr.*** is a non-canonical, single page story published in *[Hellboy Junior Halloween Special](/entry/Hellboy_Junior)*. It was written and drawn by [Bill Wray](/entry/Bill_Wray) and [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola).

## Plot Synopsis

[Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) convinces [Bill Wray](/entry/Bill_Wray) to help him make [Hellboy Junior](/entry/Hellboy_Junior) by beating him in a costumed fight.
