**Bael**, also known as **Baal**, was one of the high ranking [demons](/entry/Demon) within the Order of the Fly.

## [Hellboy in Hell](/entry/Hellboy_in_Hell)

Bael was quickly seen in a council of the Order of the Fly at [Pandemonium](/entry/Pandemonium_(location)), alongside other high-ranking demons, such as [Shax](/entry/Shax) and [Agares](/entry/Agares). He was likely killed alongside several all the other high-ranking demons after the death of Satan and uprising of the slave-demons.

## Trivia

- Bael was one of the few major demons who made the cut in the infernal council scenes. Demons like [Haborym](/entry/Haborym), Mulciber, Adramelech, [Mammon](/entry/Mammon), [Agares](/entry/Agares), Zagan, Purson, Asmoday, [Orobas](/entry/Orobas), [Moloch](/entry/Moloch), Belphegor and Belial were all planed, but did not make the Final Cut.
