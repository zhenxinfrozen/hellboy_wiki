***Return of the Lambton Worm*** is a six-page short story that originally appeared in the *Playboy* magazine. Due to the magazine's page size, it is not drawn in the standard height to width ratio.

## Synopsis

Set in 1960, North Yorkshire, England, [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) and [Professor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm) investigate the Lambton Worm. They discover that it is actually a demon. Before the demon departs, it says it will see Hellboy again in [Hell](/entry/Hell).
