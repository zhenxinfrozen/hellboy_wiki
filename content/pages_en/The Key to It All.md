***The Key to It All*** is a one-shot arc of *Hellboy in Love* written by Mike Mignola and Christopher Golden, and illustrated by Matt Smith.

## Synopsis

Anastasia’s archaeological research in to the mysterious group known as the Suaren Artea takes her to India, with Hellboy coming along for the ride. The crux of the discovery may be full of ancient bones, but the past is not as dead as they think.
