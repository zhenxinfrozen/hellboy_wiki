**Katii O’Brien** is an American comic book editor working for [Dark Horse Comics](/entry/Dark_Horse_Comics). She is editor for *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(series))* and all its related titles, as well as [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola)'s other books with the publisher.

## Education

O'Brien graduated in 2012 from Seton Hall University with a bachelors of arts in English.

## Career

O'Brien got her start in the comic book industry in September 2011 when served as an editorial intern for Marvel Comics.

O'Brien started as an assistant editor at Dark Horse Comics in May of 2017. After two an half years she was promoted to associate editor. Following that pattern she was again promoted, this time to editor, two and half years after that. She took over as editor of [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola)'s Dark Horse published books following [Scott Allie](/entry/Scott_Allie)'s departure from Dark Horse.
