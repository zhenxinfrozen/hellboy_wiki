**Agent Marble** was a field agent for the BPRD who appeared in [Hellboy II: The Golden Army](/entry/Hellboy_II%3A_The_Golden_Army).

## Biography

Marble, Steel, Flint, [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy), Liz Sherman, and [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien) were sent to investigate a black out and the supernatural attack of an auction. Marble and Hellboy separated from the others, going into the auction room. Abe revealed that the culprit had been [Tooth Fairies](/entry/Tooth_Fairies), before one assaulted Marble. Hellboy tore it off, but multiple Tooth Fairies swarmed them and stripped Marble to the bone.
