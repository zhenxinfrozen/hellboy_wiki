**Peter Johnson** is a fictional character from the *[Hellboy: Dogs of the Night](/entry/Hellboy%3A_Dogs_of_the_Night)* game.

## History

### Dogs of the Night

Peter disappeared three days ago while investigating reports of unusual happenings at a mental institution in Czechoslovakia.

Later, it turns out he was killed by one of the dogs. Hellboy and [Sara Courteville](/entry/Sara_Courteville) find his body.

## Allies and Enemies

### Allies

- **[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character))**: According to the game book, he is a friend to Hellboy, but their relationship is unknown.
- **[Sara Courteville](/entry/Sara_Courteville)**: He also knows Sarah, but not much is known about their relationship.

### Enemies

- **[Nohox Kanis](/entry/Nohox_Kanis)**: One of these demons killed him.
