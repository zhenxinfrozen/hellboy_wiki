**Simon Bruttenholm** is the uncle of [Professor Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm), great uncle of [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) (by adoption), and a member of both [the Silver Lantern Club](/entry/Silver_Lantern_Club_%28organization%29) and [the British Paranormal Society](/entry/The_British_Paranormal_Society_%28organization%29). He is a central character in [*The British Paranormal Society* series](/entry/The_British_Paranormal_Society_%28series%29).
