The **Igors** are the fictional characters from the *[Hellboy: Dogs of the Night](/entry/Hellboy%3A_Dogs_of_the_Night)* game. In the game, they are referred to only as **guards**, but in the game booklet, they are called Igors.

## History

### Dogs of the Night

These hunched servants serve the Asylum's headmaster faithfully in their garden lab. Some inmates claim to fear them, though the reasons for this are unclear (considering the traces of blood on the Igors' clothes, it can be assumed that they are beating inmates).

The Igors attempt to kill [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) but are defeated. Several Igors are found throughout the Asylum.
