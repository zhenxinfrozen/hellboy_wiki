A **Hellboy** skin was added to the fighting game *[Brawlhalla](/entry/Brawlhalla)* as a cross-promotion for [the 2019 *Hellboy* film](/entry/Hellboy_(2019_film)). Hellboy appears as an Epic Crossover skin for the character Cross.
