***The Garden (II)*** is a two-issue arc kicking off in November 2015.

## Synopsis

### Part One

Monstrosities continue to spill out of the destruction Abe saw in Florida as he meets someone with answers about his role in the worldwide disaster.

### Part Two

Abe meets a prophetic young woman who knows the truth about his role in what's coming.
