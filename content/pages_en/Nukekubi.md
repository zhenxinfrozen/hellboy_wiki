**Nukekubi** are monsters found in Japanese folklore, who appear as ordinary humans, but at night their heads detach and look for humans to consume.

In Kyoto, Japan, 1967, [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) discovered six nukekubi, whom he managed to dispatch by hiding their bodies.
