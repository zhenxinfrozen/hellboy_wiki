***The Beast of Ingelheim*** is a short story featured in the *[Hellboy Winter Special (2019)](/entry/Hellboy_Winter_Special_(2019))* written by Scott Allie and illustrated by Andrea Mutti. It was a teaser for the April 2020 miniseries *[The Last Knight of St. Hagan](/entry/The_Last_Knight_of_St._Hagan)*, which was later canceled. The story remains uncollected at this time.
