In ***Born Again***, the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense) uncovers the bones of a creature defeated long ago.

## Publication History

*Born Again* was originally published as part of *Hellboy Premiere Edition* in March 2004. The issue, an exclusive published through *Wizard* magazine \#148, also contained the [*Hellboy*](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29) story *[The Penanggalan](/entry/The_Penanggalan)*. The issue was published with three covers, one by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) showing an image from *The Penanggalan*, one by [Guy Davis](/entry/Guy_Davis) showing the members of the B.P.R.D. and [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29), and a cover (exclusive to Wizard World in Los Angeles) showing a picture of [Ron Perlman](/entry/Ron_Perlman) as Hellboy.

*Born Again* was collected as the prologue in the trade paperback *[B.P.R.D.: The Dead](/entry/Trade_Collections)* in September 2005.

## Synopsis

[Elizabeth Sherman](/entry/Elizabeth_Sherman), [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien), [Roger](/entry/Roger) and [Johann Kraus](/entry/Johann_Kraus) break their way into a cavern under a suburb of Chicago. Inside they find the skeleton of a large dinosaur like creature. Johann sends some of his ectoplasm into he bones. The creature says it was killed by [Shonchin](/entry/Shonchin). Now that its body and spirit are reconnected it overpowers Johann. Unable to stop it, Liz is forced to burn the creature. Johann, back in control, is able to reform himself and re inhabit his containment suit. Johann speculates that Shonchin must have been a shaman that defeated the creature long ago. Roger finds a [Spiked Cylinder](/entry/Spiked_Cylinder) in the cave and takes it with him.
