***The Penanggalan*** is a short story in which [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) faces a Malaysian demon.

## Publication History

*The Penanggalan* was originally published as part of *[Hellboy Premiere Edition](/entry/Hellboy_Premiere_Edition)* in March 2004. It was subsequently reprinted in *[Hellboy Comic Con Breda 2010](/entry/Hellboy_Comic_Con_Breda_2010)*.

*The Penanggalan* was reprinted, with small changes,[1] as part of [*Hellboy – Volume 7: The Troll Witch and Others*](/entry/Trade_Collections) in October 2007.

A digital version of this comic was also included on the DVD of *[Hellboy: Blood and Iron](/entry/Hellboy%3A_Blood_and_Iron)*.

## Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) travels Malaysia in [1958](/entry/Hellboy_Universe_timeline). A Malay girl tells Hellboy that there was once an old woman who got so startled that she kicked her own head off. The woman's head and organs flew out of her body and became a demon, the first Penanggalan. The girl continues, that once the people respected the local [Penanggalan](/entry/Penanggalan) and were able to pacify her. However, the older generation is gone and the Penanggalan has returned.

The girl takes Hellboy to the Penanggalan's cave. Hellboy sees the body of a Penanggalan, but realizes that it cannot be the current demon plaguing the area and that there must be another. He turns to see the headless body of the girl fall to the ground. The new Penanggalan attacks Hellboy, tangling him in her organs. Hellboy leaps outside as the sun rises, destroying the demon.

## Story Chronology

The story takes place in 1958, the same year as *[The Crooked Man](/entry/The_Crooked_Man_%28comic_story%29)*.
