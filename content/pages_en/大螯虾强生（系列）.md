![](/content/images/Front_Page_-_Lobster_Johnson.webp) ***Lobster Johnson*** is a line of comic books about the character [The Lobster](/entry/Lobster_Johnson_%28character%29). It is a spinoff of the *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29)* series.

## Publication History

The *Lobster Johnson* series made its debut with a short backup story, *[The Killer in My Skull](/entry/The_Killer_in_My_Skull)*, in [*Hellboy: Box Full of Evil* \#1](/entry/Box_Full_of_Evil). This story would later be collected in the [first volume of the B.P.R.D. trades](/entry/TPB).

In September 2007, the *Lobster Johnson* debuted its first full miniseries, *[The Iron Prometheus](/entry/The_Iron_Prometheus)*, written by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) with art by [Jason Armstrong](/entry/Jason_Armstrong). Set in 1937, this story explored [Memnan Saa](/entry/Memnan_Saa), setting up plot elements for *[B.P.R.D.: The Black Goddess](/entry/The_Black_Goddess)*.

In January 2012, *Lobster Johnson* returned for *[The Burning Hand](/entry/The_Burning_Hand)*, a five-issue miniseries that takes place in 1932 at the beginning of the Lobster's career. [John Arcudi](/entry/John_Arcudi) became the ongoing writer for the series with this title, saying that future *Lobster Johnson* stories would continue chronologically from that point. This is mostly true, although the one-shot stories often jump back and forth as much as a year. However, they are collected chronologically in the trade paperback collections.

*The Burning Hand* also saw [Tonci Zonjic](/entry/Tonci_Zonjic) join *[Lobster Johnson](/entry/Lobster_Johnson)* as the primary artist on the series. He draws most of the longer arcs, while guest artists usually draw the one-shot stories.

A full list of *Lobster Johnson* stories can be found here: [List of **Lobster Johnson** stories](/entry/%3ACategory%3ALobster_Johnson_story)

### Trade Paperbacks
