**Professor Onchukov** was a Russian scholar who worked with the [Bureau for Paranormal Research and Defense](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense).

## History

Previously an affiliate of the [B.P.R.D](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense), in 1967, Professor Onchukov arranged the B.P.R.D to have [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) meet in at Goloski Train Station in Russia claiming to have information. However instead of meeting Onchukov, he found [Yad Tovich](/entry/Yad_Tovich) with Onchukov's head, now a werewolf. The hunter informed the paranormal investigator that the good professor was actually going to murder Hellboy as their was a bounty on his head after he killed [Baba Yaga](/entry/Baba_Yaga_(character)). Yad planned to sell Onchukov's head to a buyer.

## Powers & Abilities

Professor Onchukov sold his soul to the devil to change into a wolf. Even when he was beheaded Onchukov's still maintained his wolf-like appearance.
