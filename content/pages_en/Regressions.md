***Regressions*** is a two-issue arc kicking off in April 2016.

## Synopsis

### Part One

[Abe](/entry/Abraham_Sapien) arrives in New York City to discover the [Bureau](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense) knew more about his origins than they let on.

### Part Two

Abe discovers parts of the biggest answers, withheld from him for decades by the BPRD itself.
