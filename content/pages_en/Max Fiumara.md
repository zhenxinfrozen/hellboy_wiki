**Max Fiumara** is a comics artist. He made his Hellboy Universe debut in May 2012 with [*B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth—The Transformation of J.H. O'Donnell*](/entry/The_Transformation_of_J.H._O%27Donnell). He was one of two artists that worked on the [*Dark and Terrible* cycle](/entry/Dark_and_Terrible_%28story_cycle%29) of [*Abe Sapien*](/entry/Abe_Sapien_%28series%29).
