**William Bright** was a member of the [Heliopic Brotherhood of Ra](/entry/Heliopic_Brotherhood_of_Ra).

## History

As a member of the Brotherhood, Bright was obsessed with knowing the "true history of the world." Inspired by John Dee's black mirror and Charles Hinton's ideas of a fourth dimension, Bright created a scrying apparatus, which will allow him to astral project himself through time to witness the past.
