***The Haunted Boy*** is a one-shot story written by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) and [John Arcudi](/entry/John_Arcudi) with art by [Patric Reynolds](/entry/Patric_Reynolds) and a cover by [Dave Johnson](/entry/Dave_Johnson).

## Publication History

*Abe Sapien: The Haunted Boy* was published as a one-shot issue in October 2009. The story was collected, along with *[The Abyssal Plain](/entry/The_Abyssal_Plain)* and *[The Devil Does Not Jest](/entry/The_Devil_Does_Not_Jest)*, in the trade *[Abe Sapien – Volume 2: The Devil Does Not Jest and Other Stories](/entry/Trade_Collections)*.

## Synopsis

In his earliest days as an agent of the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense), [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abraham_Sapien) is sent on a simple mission to investigate a haunting following the death of a young boy. What he finds there is far stranger and more deadly.
