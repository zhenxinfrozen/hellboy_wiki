**Pernilla Aickman** was the daughter of [Edmond Aickman](/entry/Edmond_Aickman). She first appeared in the 2001 prose novel, *[Hellboy: The Bones of Giants](/entry/Hellboy%3A_The_Bones_of_Giants_(prose_novel))*. She appeared in the comics later when the novel was adapted [for the novel's twentieth anniversary](/entry/The_Bones_of_Giants_(comic_miniseries)).
