The town called **Griart** was once known as **Saint August**. The inhabitants changed the name of the town and demolished the castle's bell tower to avoid a massacre by the Inquisition after a traveling monk cursed the ruling family for deviating from what he considered good faith.

The ruse worked, but [in 1994](/entry/Wake_the_Devil) the town was massacred by a werewolf and the spirits of his kin that had fallen under his thrall.
