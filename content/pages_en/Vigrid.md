**Vigrid** is key location during [Ragna Rok](/entry/Ragna_Rok_(event)), where an important battle will take place.

## History

When [Nimue](/entry/Nimue) became possessed by the [Ogdru Jahad](/entry/Ogdru_Jahad), she/the dragon commanded an army of monsters and the [Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse](/entry/Four_Horsemen_of_the_Apocalypse) to go furth to the world Human and "kill them all".

Luckily George Washbook, armed with [Excalibur](/entry/Excalibur) and leading the dead nobility of England, confronted the army of monsters, killing them. Meanwhile [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) battled with Nimue/Ogdru Jahad, defeating the dragon, but dying himself in the process. [King Vold](/entry/King_Vold_(character)) also appeared on the scene.

![](/content/images/Vigrid.jpeg)

While the forces of good prevailed, this was only the beginning of the End Times, with the [Ogdru Hem](/entry/Ogdru_Hem) starting to emerge in rapid numbers after the battle and most of Great Britain being wiped of the map.

## Description

*"This field... it is called Vigrid. And it's written in the stars and the roots of trees... when Ragna Rok comes it will be here... The [champion of man](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) will battle the [dragon](/entry/Ogdru_Jahad)... and that will be the end."*
