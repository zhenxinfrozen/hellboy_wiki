***Family Ties*** is a one-shot arc of *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.](/entry/Hellboy_and_the_B.P.R.D.): 1957* written by Mike Mignola and Chris Roberson, and illustrated by Laurence Campbell.

## Synopsis

Before they can track down an errant copy of [Gustav Strobl](/entry/Gustav_Strobl)’s *Witchcraft and Demonology*, Hellboy and B.P.R.D. agent Susan Xiang are sidetracked by a beset housewife’s pleas for help. Unraveling the mystery of an unwanted house guest may lead the occult investigators right to where they wanted to be, but also right into demonic danger.
