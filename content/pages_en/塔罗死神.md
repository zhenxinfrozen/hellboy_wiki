***The Death Card*** is a one-shot in the [*Hellboy in Hell* series](/entry/Hellboy_in_Hell_%28series%29).

## Publication History

The story is scheduled to come out in May 2014.

## Synopsis

While sitting down with Mr. Dean and Jenks to discuss the utterly pointless tasks to create a map of Hell, Hellboy finds himself having a card game against who he failed to recognize as a [vampire he killed years ago in Prague](/entry/The_Vampire_of_Prague).
