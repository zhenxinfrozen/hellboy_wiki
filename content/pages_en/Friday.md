***Friday*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#6](/entry/Hellboy%3A_Weird_Tales)*, written by [Doug Petrie](/entry/Doug_Petrie) with art provided by [Gene Colan](/entry/Gene_Colan).

## Plot Synopsis

[Elizabeth Sherman](/entry/Elizabeth_Sherman) and [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien) take care of a kidnapping case at an aquarium since [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) is unavailable. Following the successful rescue of the kidnapped child the two [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) agents go get coffee together which is just what Liz needed since she was in a lonely mood.
