**Dragons** are Reptilian creatures infamous in Human cultures across the world. They are seen as possessing supernatural powers and abilities, and sometimes even capable of speaking Human languages. Though they may seem similar to the [Ogdru Jahad](/entry/Ogdru_Jahad) and their spawn, they aren't directly related in anyway.
