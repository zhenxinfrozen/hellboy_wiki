**Morgan Kaler** was a man living in Utah in 1880s, appearing in the Witchfinder story *[Lost and Gone Forever](/entry/Lost_and_Gone_Forever)*.

## History

Morgan Kaler assisted [Sir Edward Grey](/entry/Sir_Edward_Grey) in his search for a [Lord Glaren](/entry/Lord_Glaren), an influential member of the [Heliopic Brotherhood of Ra](/entry/Heliopic_Brotherhood_of_Ra), but Kaler only did so as he believed something further was afoot after several people vanished after they were locked in a nearby church.

After surviving several attacks by zombies and a stone dog (mainly due to Kaler's expertise) the two came to the conclusion that a witch by the name of [Eris](/entry/Eris) was responsible for the disappearance of the church people and the zombie attacks. With the guidance of a Native American spirit, Grey managed to destroy the source of Eris' power and thus defeats her. Afterwards Kaler and Grey parted ways, likely to never see one another again.

In *[B.P.R.D.: 1948](/entry/1948)* a local bar in Utah was named after a Kaler, it is unknown if this was after Morgan or one of his descendants.
