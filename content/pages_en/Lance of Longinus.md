**The Lance of Longinus** or the Holy Lance is a powerful magical weapon imbued with the blood of Christ. Anyone who possesses the spear can become invincible

## Description

Near the end of WW2, Hitler got hold of the Lance and decided to use to turn the tide of the war but the BPRD agents got to him and killed him and took back the lance.

The Lance is now stored in the storage of the BPRD HQ.
