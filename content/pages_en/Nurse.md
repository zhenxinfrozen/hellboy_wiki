The **Nurse** is a fictional character from the *[Hellboy: Dogs of the Night](/entry/Hellboy%3A_Dogs_of_the_Night)* game. She fight using her truncheon.

## History

### Dogs of the Night

Once normal medical orderly. According to the game book, some of the nurses have been corrupted by the evil forces unleashed by the portal, and turned into mindless servants.

She encountered [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) in one of the Asylum's rooms. At first, she tried to drive him away, but attacked him when he refused. He ultimately defeated her.
