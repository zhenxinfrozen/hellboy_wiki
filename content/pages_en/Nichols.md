Agent **Nichols** is a field agent for the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense) He has been with the Bureau since the [Nebraska incident](/entry/Katha-Hem) in March 2006. He was trained by veteran Bureau member [Pauline Raskin](/entry/Pauline_Raskin) .

## *B.P.R.D. Hell on Earth: [The Long Death](/entry/The_Long_Death)*

Agent Nichols first appears in the B.P.R.D. Hell on Earth story: *The Long Death*. He is part of a team assembled by Agent [Johann Kraus](/entry/Johann_Kraus) to investigate the slaughter of a family and park ranger in the woods of British Columbia.

## *B.P.R.D. Hell on Earth: [Wasteland](/entry/Wasteland)*

Nichols reappears in the B.P.R.D. Hell on Earth story: *Wasteland*.  Nichols, along with a few other field agents, are lead into the destroyed city of Chicago by Johann Kraus in order to locate a missing B.P.R.D. team. During the mission he becomes convinced that it is the end, and begins to takes pills to “take the edge off of things.” The pills however dulled his senses to such an extent he starts to lose concentration during the field.

## *<u>B.P.R.D. Hell o</u>*<u>n</u> Earth: Metomorphis

Nichols is among the agents lead by Kraus to destroying monsters protected by a local cult. The conflict leaves Nichols' fellow agent [Enos](/entry/Enos) dead. Taking advantage of the situation Kraus possesses Enos' dead body to neutralise the armed cultist, but his actions have a negative response from his fellow agents, Nicholas in particular. On the helicopter ride back Nichols snaps and grabs Kraus' spare suit and flings it out in mid-flight.
