***Downtime*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#3](/entry/Hellboy__Weird_Tales)*, with both the story and art curtesy of [Bob Fingerman](/entry/Bob_Fingerman).

## Plot Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)), [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien) and [Roger](/entry/Roger) deal with the day to day issues and inconveniences of working in an office.
