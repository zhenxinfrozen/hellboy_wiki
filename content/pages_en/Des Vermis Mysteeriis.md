**Des Vermis Mysteeriis** is a grimoire that contained knowledge of the [Ogdru Jahad](/entry/Ogdru_Jahad) on page 87.

## Content

*"In the coldest regions of space, the monstrous entities [Ogdru Jahad](/entry/Ogdru_Jahad) - the Seven Gods of Chaos - slumber in their crystal prison, waiting to reclaim Earth... and burn the heavens"*

## Trivia

- This is a fictional version of the book De Vermis Mysteriis, which was created by Robert Bloch, and was later incorporated by H. P. Lovecraft into the lore of the Cthulhu Mythos.
