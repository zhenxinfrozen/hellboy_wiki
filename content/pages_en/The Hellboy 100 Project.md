***The Hellboy 100 Project*** was a book published by [Dark Horse Comics](/entry/Dark_Horse_Comics) as a fundraiser for the Hero Initiative. The book collects 109 variant covers each created by a different artist. Each artist drew directly on a blank cover version of [*Hellboy and the B.P.R.D: 1954* \#1](/entry/1952). The individual issues were then auctioned off on eBay with the proceeds also going to the Hero Initiative.

## Publishers Summary

*In the now-grand tradition of Hero Initiative’s previous and wildly successful 100 Project books, Mike Mignola’s signature creation comes to the fore! ALL of Hero’s original covers to Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.: 1952 \#1 are on display in this great book, with art by [John Cassaday](/entry/John_Cassaday), Francis Manapul, Todd Nauck, Whilce Portacio, [Eric Powell](/entry/Eric_Powell), [Paolo Rivera](/entry/Paolo_Rivera), Bill Sienkiewicz, and of course, [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola)!*

## Publication History

*The Hellboy 100 Project* was published as both a hardcover and a paperback on the same day, September 23, 2015.
