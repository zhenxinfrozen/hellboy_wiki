![](/content/images/Front_Page_-_Witchfinder.png) ***Sir Edward Grey: Witchfinder***is a line of comic books about the character [Sir Edward Grey](/entry/Sir_Edward_Grey). It is a spinoff of the*[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29)'' series. The series is more commonly referred to as simply***Witchfinder**''.

## Publication History

Sir Edward Grey was first mentioned in *[Hellboy: Wake the Devil](/entry/Wake_the_Devil)* and since then has had a number of relatively minor appearances in various *Hellboy* comics, sometimes appearing as a cloaked figure wearing a mask. Grey was featured prominently in the first issue of *[Abe Sapien: The Drowning](/entry/The_Drowning)*, first published in February of 2008*.*

Later that same year, the *Witchfinder* series made its debut with the short story *[Murderous Intent](/entry/Murderous_Intent)* in *MySpace Dark Horse Presents* \#16. This short story was designed to introduce Sir Edward Grey in advance of the miniseries *[In the Service of Angels](/entry/In_the_Service_of_Angels)*. Both stories were written by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) with [Ben Stenbeck](/entry/Ben_Stenbeck) on art.

The second miniseries, *[Lost and Gone Forever](/entry/Lost_and_Gone_Forever)*, came out in 2011, this time with [John Arcudi](/entry/John_Arcudi) handling writing duties and [John Severin](/entry/John_Severin) on art. This was Severin's last complete comic before he died.

The third miniseries, *[The Mysteries of Unland](/entry/The_Mysteries_of_Unland)*, was published in 2014. This story is unique among the *Witchfinder* titles in that it does not give Mike Mignola a writing credit. *The Mysteries of Unland* was written by [Kim Newman](/entry/Kim_Newman) and [Maura McHugh](/entry/Maura_McHugh) with [Tyler Crook](/entry/Tyler_Crook) on art duties. The final pages of this story tie into a flashback sequence in *[Abe Sapien: The Shadow Over Suwanee](/entry/The_Shadow_Over_Suwanee)*, also drawn by Crook.

*[Beware the Ape](/entry/Beware_the_Ape)*, a short story set in between *Lost and Gone Forever* and *The Mysteries of Unland*, came in May 2014 in *Dark Horse Presents* \#36. This was written by Mike Mignola with art by Ben Stenbeck. Though only a short story, it ties in with the larger mythology of a recurring Cthulhu-like statues (seen in *[Lobster Johnson: Tony Masso's Finest Hour](/entry/Tony_Masso%27s_Finest_Hour)* and several *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.](/entry/Hellboy_and_the_B.P.R.D.)* stories).

In 2016, [Chris Roberson](/entry/Chris_Roberson) became the ongoing series writer for *Witchfinder* beginning with *[City of the Dead](/entry/City_of_the_Dead)* (with Ben Stenbeck on art duties). Mike Mignola was so impressed with Roberson's work on the title, he was invited to write several other titles as well.

A full list of *Witchfinder* stories can be found here: *[List of **Witchfinder** stories](/entry/%3ACategory%3AWitchfinder_story)*

## Collections

*see also [Witchfinder Trade Paperback Collections](/entry/TPB)*

All in-continuity *Witchfinder* stories are collected into trade paperbacks eventually. So far there have been six volumes. The standard trades usually contain a sketchbook or gallery with an afterword by the author. The series is also collected in [omnibus editions](/entry/Omnibus_Editions).
