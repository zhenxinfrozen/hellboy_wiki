***Huge Retarded Duck*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy Junior \#2](/entry/Hellboy_Junior)*, parodying the Harvey Comics character Baby Huey. It was written by [Bill Wray](/entry/Bill_Wray) with [Stephen DeStefano](/entry/Stephen_DeStefano) on art duty.

## Plot Synopsis

Baby runs away from home after his parents, Mama and Papa, get in a fight over his inability not to poop his diaper. During the fight Mama servers Papa's spine. Baby is clueless about how to live on on his own and after asking a trio of other young ducks to help him they instead abuse him. Mama attempts to rescue Baby but the trio of ducks overpower her, grope her and forcibly undress her. This enrages Baby and proceeds to murder the trio. After he calms down he becomes sexually aroused seeing Mama in an undressed state. Since Papa is no longer able to perform his "husbandly duties" Mama agrees to take Baby as her new lover and they proceed to procreate.
