***Flight Risk*** was a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#2](/entry/Hellboy%3A_Weird_Tales)*, written by [Joe Casey](/entry/Joe_Casey) and art by [Steve Parkhouse](/entry/Steve_Parkhouse).

## Plot Synopsis

Lloyd, a [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) agent, attempts to reclaim his Freestyle Altitude Record. During his attempt he is attacked by giant desert bats and is forced to abandon his jetpack while in midair. He is also perform an emergency landing in a body of water after the bats shred his parachute. While he does reclaim his record he decides that this has been his last flight and that he doesn't want his name associated with the record any longer.
