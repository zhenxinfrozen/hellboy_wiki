**John Lindley Byrne** is a British-born Canadian-American comic book writer and aritst, best known for his work on Marvel Comics' *X-Men* and *Fantastic Four* and the 1986 relaunch of DC Comics' *Superman* franchise.

## *Hellboy* Bibliography

Byrne wrote the scripts for the three earliest *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29)* stories;

- [*Hellboy* promotional comic](/entry/World%27s_Greatest_Paranormal_Investigator_%28SDCC%29) (*San Diego Comic Con Comics* \#2)
- [*Hellboy* promotional comic](/entry/World%27s_Greatest_Paranormal_Investigator_%28CBG%29) (*The Comic Buyer's Guide* \#1070)
- *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29): [Seed of Destruction](/entry/Seed_of_Destruction)* \#1–4

### Related Works

John Byrne and [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) were both published under [Dark Horse Comics' "Legend" imprint](/entry/Dark_Horse_Comics) from 1994 through 1998. During this time, they briefly explored crossing over their characters to create a shared universe, resulting in *Hellboy* characters appearing in the following comics written and drawn by Byrne:

- [*John Byrne's Next Men* \#21](/entry/Next_Men__Faith_%E2%80%93_Part_3) ([Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) appears in a sequence drawn by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola))
- *Danger Unlimited* \#4 (Hellboy cameos in a scene alongside other superheroes)
- *[Babe 2](/entry/Babe_2)* \#1–2 (featuring [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abraham_Sapien); Hellboy appears in a postscript scene)

Byrne's *Danger Unlimited* characters [The Torch of Liberty](/entry/The_Torch_of_Liberty) and Dr. Robert Carson made brief appearances in early *Hellboy* stories (*Seed of Destruction* and *[The Chained Coffin](/entry/The_Chained_Coffin)*, respectively).
