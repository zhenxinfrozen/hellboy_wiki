***A Chain Forged in Life*** is a story takes place in the middle of [the Lobster's](/entry/The_Lobster) career as a crime fighter in the 1930s.

## Publication History

This one-shot was published in July 2015, featuring a cover by Tonči Zonjić.

## Synopsis

Santa is in trouble! Santa was taken hostage after witnessing a hostile robbery and the death of a man. Hidden away in a mountain cabin, will the [Lobster](/entry/The_Lobster) be able to find Santa and save Christmas?
