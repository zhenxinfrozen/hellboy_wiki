**St. Agnes Church** is a church in Shropshire, England.

## 1886

Sir [Edward Grey](/entry/Edward_Grey) interned the body and soul of a [millenia old witch](/entry/Boudicca's_Hag) in the basement of the church and subsequently hired the Burke family to watch over the grounds and contact him or the next witchfinder if the remains are disturbed.

## (/entry/The_Witch_Tree)

By 1953, the remains of the witch were stolen by a Shropshire local, Jaime Moleworth, but was witnessed by Sam Burke.  Burke then went on to contact Professor [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm) and [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) to deal with the dilemma.
