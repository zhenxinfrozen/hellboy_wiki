**Mitri** is a fictional character from the *[Hellboy: Dogs of the Night](/entry/Hellboy__Dogs_of_the_Night)* game. He is a brother of [Kalin](/entry/Kalin).

## History

### Dogs of the Night

The elder of twin brothers born nearly 750 years ago and abandoned on the steps of a monastery. Mitri became an accomplished scholar, but his kind heart prevented him from tapping into the dark powers employed by Kalin.

When Kalin embraced the ancient evil of the [Nohox Kanis](/entry/Nohox_Kanis) and has allied himself with them to create a portal between Hell and Earth, Mitri closed the portal nearly 700 years ago.
