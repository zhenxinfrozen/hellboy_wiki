The **Hydra** is a serpentine monster from Ancient Greek, which had the power of regenerating two heads for every head which was destroyed.

Hellboy managed to defeat the creature by tying its necks in a knot and the creature was contained by the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.).
