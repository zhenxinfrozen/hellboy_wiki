**Doug Jones** (born May 24, 1960) is the American actor who plays [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien) in both *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28film%29)* (with *[David Hyde Pierce](/entry/David_Hyde_Pierce)* doing the voice) and *[Hellboy II: The Golden Army](/entry/Hellboy_II%3A_The_Golden_Army)* (where he voices the character himself). He also voices the character in the animated features *[Blood and Iron](/entry/Blood_and_Iron)* and *[Sword of Storms](/entry/Sword_of_Storms)* and the video game [Hellboy: The Science of Evil](/entry/Hellboy%3A_The_Science_of_Evil).

In *Hellboy II: The Golden Army* he also plays the Angel of Death and the Chamberlain.

## Notable Roles

- Abe Sapien in *Hellboy* (2004)
- Cesare in *The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari* (2005)
- Fauno and Pale Man in *Pan's Labyrinth*(2006)
- [Silver Surfer](/entry/w%3Ac%3Amarvel%3ANorrin_Radd_%28Earth-616%29) in *[Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer](/entry/w%3Ac%3Amarvel-movies%3AFantastic_Four%3A_Rise_of_the_Silver_Surfer)* (2007)
- Abe Sapien, The Angel of Death and The Chamberlain in *Hellboy II: The Golden Army*(2008)
- La Gueule in *Gainsbourg* (2010)
- The Ancient in *The Strain* (2014 - 2016)
- Amphibian Man in *The Shape of Water* (2017)
- Commander Saru in *Star Trek: Discovery* (2017-present)
