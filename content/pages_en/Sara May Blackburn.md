**Sara May Blackburn** first appeared as a young girl with precognitive abilities in [*Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.: The Return of Effie Kolb*](/entry/The_Return_of_Effie_Kolb), set in 1967. She is the main character in the miniseries *[Castle Full of Blackbirds](/entry/Castle_Full_of_Blackbirds)*. She also made a brief appearance in *[The Serpent in the Garden: Ed Grey and the Last Battle for England](/entry/The_Serpent_in_the_Garden%3A_Ed_Grey_and_the_Last_Battle_for_England)*.
