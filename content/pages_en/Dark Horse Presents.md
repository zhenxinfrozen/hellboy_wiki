Dark Horse Presents was an anthology title published by Dark Horse. The title ran for 157 issues, with a number of specials and annuals, between July 1986 and September 2000. The second incarnation was published on MySpace, with 36 issues running from July 2007 until August 2010. A third incarnation under "volume 2" began in April 2011, released in print form once again with 36 titles. Beginning in August 2014, volume 3 of Dark Horse Presents returned as a forty-eight-page book. This volume ended in 2017 with issue 33.

A number of issues contained [Hellboy stories](/entry/Hellboy_Comics) including;

- *Dark Horse Presents* \#89 (Sep '94) - "[The Wolves of Saint August](/entry/The_Wolves_of_Saint_August)" 2

- *Dark Horse Presents* \#91 (Nov '94) - "[The Wolves of Saint August](/entry/The_Wolves_of_Saint_August)" 4
- *Dark Horse Presents* \#100-0[1] (Jul '95) - a 3 page teaser of "[The Chained Coffin](/entry/The_Chained_Coffin)"
- *Dark Horse Presents* \#100-2 (Aug '95) - "[The Chained Coffin](/entry/The_Chained_Coffin)"

- *Dark Horse Presents Vol. 2* \#8/ \#177 (Feb '12) - "[An Unmarked Grave](/entry/An_Unmarked_Grave)"
- *Dark Horse Presents Vol. 2* \#31 / \#200 (Dec '13) - "Hellboy get Married" 1
