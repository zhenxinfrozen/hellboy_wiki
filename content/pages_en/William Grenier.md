**William Grenier** was a member of the royal family of Grenier Prince of the Griart and a werewolf who survived the slaughter from in St. August.

## History

William Grenier is an old [werewolf](/entry/Werewolves) who kills villagers in an abandoned town in Eastern Europe and also [Father Kelly](/entry/Father_Kelly), an old friend of [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)), who investigated the incidents. In a long battle, Hellboy defeats him.
