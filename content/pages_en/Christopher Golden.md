**Christopher Golden** is an author and editor who has written or co-written both *Hellboy* prose and comics. Additionally, he and [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) have collaborated on a number of other projects. Golden also contributed to a number of early versions of the script to the [2019 *Hellboy* film](/entry/%E5%9C%B0%E7%8B%B1%E7%94%B7%E7%88%B5%EF%BC%9A%E8%A1%80%E7%9A%87%E5%90%8E%E5%B4%9B%E8%B5%B7)) although he ultimately went uncredited.

## *Hellboy* Universe Bibliography

Golden has written a number of comics, three [*Hellboy* novels](/entry/Hellboy_Universe_novels) and one short story. Additionally, he has edited every [*Hellboy* prose short story anthology](/entry/Hellboy_Universe_novels).
