***Party Pooper*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#1](/entry/Hellboy__Weird_Tales)*, with both art and story by [Andi Watson](/entry/Andi_Watson).

## Plot Synopsis

[Katherine Corrigan](/entry/%E5%87%AF%E7%89%B9%C2%B7%E8%80%83%E7%91%9E%E6%A0%B9)) takes [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) out for the day while [Elizabeth Sherman](/entry/Elizabeth_Sherman) and [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien) prepare a surprise birthday party for him back at [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) headquarters; complete with a pile of pancakes toped with a candle. Little does everyone else realize that Hellboy is struggling to come to terms with his birth and his fate.
