**Enos** is a recurring character in the *[B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth](/entry/BPRD%3F%E4%BA%BA%E9%97%B4%E5%9C%B0%E7%8B%B1))* story cycle. He first appeared in the arc *[Wasteland](/entry/Wasteland)* as a B.P.R.D. agent, part of the team that recovers [Agent Ted Howards](/entry/Ted_Howards). While his given name was never in the comics, it appeared as an Easter egg in the [2019 *Hellboy* film](/entry/%E5%9C%B0%E7%8B%B1%E7%94%B7%E7%88%B5%EF%BC%9A%E8%A1%80%E7%9A%87%E5%90%8E%E5%B4%9B%E8%B5%B7)).

## History

Throughout the *Hell on Earth* cycle, Enos was frequently seen in the company of Agents [Johann Kraus](/entry/Johann_Kraus) and [Nichols](/entry/Nichols) until his eventual death in *[Nowhere, Nothing, Never](/entry/Nowhere%2C_Nothing%2C_Never)* fighting armed civilians that were protecting monsters. After his death, Agent Kraus possessed his corpse to defeat the armed locals. However Kraus's actions discomforted his fellow agents who from then on distanced themselves from him.

## Personality

Enos seems to have a somewhat unprofessional attitude to his job, often joking about the impending doom he often find him self in and once even cheering on an interdimensional monster as it battled an [Ogdru Hem](/entry/Ogdru_Hem). Enos is usually seen armed with heavy weaponry such as grenade launchers.
