**Lord Michael Glaren** was a high ranking member of the [Heliopic Brotherhood of Ra](/entry/Heliopic_Brotherhood_of_Ra) (HBR) and the younger brother of [Lord Adam Glaren](/entry/Adam_Glaren). After Adam's death in 1880, Michael took over looking after [Panya](/entry/Panya_%28character%29). She was allowed to explore intellectually and became involved in many of the HBR's science and paranormal experiments. However, she began to grow tired of being cooped indoors and decided to take some time away, but Lord Glaren refused to let her leave. Soon after Panya was promised freedom in America by the [Oannes Society](/entry/Oannes_Society), and escaped from the HBR with them. She never saw Lord Glaren again.
