***Children of the Black Mound*** was a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#1](/entry/Hellboy__Weird_Tales)*, written by [Fabian Nicieza](/entry/Fabian_Nicieza) and drawn by [Stefano Raffaele](/entry/Stefano_Raffaele).

## Plot Synopsis

A disagreement is taking place in The Theological Seminary of Tbilisi in 1896 about how much stock should be placed in in folklore and mythologies by members of the Orthodox Church. A story about [Baba Yaga](/entry/Baba_Yaga_%28character%29) stealing the children of a small town is used as talking point in the debate. The story ends with Baba Yaga commenting that both her time and that of the Orthodox Church are coming to a close due to the rise of communism in Eastern Europe.
