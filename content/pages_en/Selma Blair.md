**Selma Blair** (b. 1972), is the American actress who plays Liz Sherman. She has performed the role in the 2004 *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(2004_film))* movie and its sequel *[Hellboy II: The Golden Army](/entry/Hellboy_II__The_Golden_Army)*. She provided the voice for the character in the Hellboy animated films: *Blood and Iron* and *Sword of Storms*.

## Notable Roles

- Vivian Kensington in *Legally Blonde* (2001)
- Zoe Bean in *Zoe, Duncan, Jack and Jane* (1999-2000)
- Cecile Caldwell in *Cruel Intentions* (1999)
