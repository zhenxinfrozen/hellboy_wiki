**Alise Gluškova** is a Latvian comics artist. She made her Hellboy Universe debut in [*Abe Sapien: The Calm Before the Storm*](/entry/The_Calm_Before_the_Storm), originally a fan work, but later canonized when [Scott Allie](/entry/Scott_Allie) wrote the dialogue for it.
