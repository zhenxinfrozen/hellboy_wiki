**Orobas** was a [demon](/entry/Demon) and prince of [Hell](/entry/Hell).

In the 19th Century the demon was summoned by [Gustav Strobl](/entry/Gustav_Strobl) in an abandoned cathedral in France. Strobl originally thought Orobas was [Satan](/entry/Satan) himself, and begged the demon to led him lead Satan's army during the apocalypse. Orobas laughed at Strobl's request, stating that he can offer only Earthly gifts.

Following the death of Satan, Orobas was one of the many demons killed in Hell's civil war, alongside [Beleth](/entry/Beleth), [Shax](/entry/Shax), [Amdusias](/entry/Amdusias), [Rumael](/entry/Rumael) and [Moloch](/entry/Moloch).
