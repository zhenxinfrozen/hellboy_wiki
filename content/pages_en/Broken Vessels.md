***Broken Vessels*** originally appeared in the *[Hellboy Winter Special (2016)](/entry/Hellboy_Winter_Special_%282016%29)*. It is the Hellboy Universe debut of Tim Sale. The story focuses on [Gall Dennar](/entry/Gall_Dennar) in prehistoric times.

## Synopsis

While camping on a snow-covered plain, Gall Dennar is approached by a man wearing a robe of skulls. The skulls belonged to shamans capable of harnessing the [Vril](/entry/Vril). The man had dug them up, hoping to learn its use. He claimed the skulls whispered to him how the Vril was used to make [Hyperborea](/entry/Hyperboreans) a paradise.

As the man speaks he is surrounded by faceless specters. Each grabs a skull which they place over their face. As the specters fly away, the man dies, declaring that it shows the Vril power remains, but that it wasn't meant for man.
