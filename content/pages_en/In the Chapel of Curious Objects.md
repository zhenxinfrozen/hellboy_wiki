***In the Chapel of Curious Objects*** is the coda to *[The Amazing Screw-On Head and Other Curious Objects](/entry/The_Amazing_Screw-On_Head_and_Other_Curious_Objects)*. In the end notes for the book [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) states that it isn't really a story.

## Plot

Within a chapel, in a familiar location, a hand is seen opening a hidden door on a statue. Behind the hidden door resides the [Solid Objects](/entry/The_Magician_and_the_Snake).

## Connections to Other Mignola Works

### Amazing Screw On Head

- The chapel is located in the same place Emperor Zombie flew his Evil Skull Balloon over on his way to the Aswam Valley in *[The Amazing Screw-On Head](/entry/The_Amazing_Screw-On_Head)*.

### The Prisoner of Mars

- The same skull with lightbulb in its left eye socket and a "61" on it forehead seen amongst the possessions of Professor Cyclops in *[The Prisoner of Mars](/entry/The_Prisoner_of_Mars)* is seen in the chapel.

### The Magician and the Snake

- The same statue of the Magician, the Snake, and the Solid Objects, seen at the at the end *[The Magician and the Snake](/entry/The_Magician_and_the_Snake)* is the statue housing the Solid Objects in the chapel.

### Abu Gung and the Beanstalk

- Within *The Chapel of Curious Objects* it is not made clear who the hand opening the statue belongs to. All that is discernible is that the hand belongs to an oder individual of lighter complexion. Immediately following the story within *[The Amazing Screw-On Head and Other Curious Objects](/entry/The_Amazing_Screw-On_Head_and_Other_Curious_Objects)* is a portrait of an elderly Professor E.E. Stoop. This might suggest that he is the individual opening the statue.
