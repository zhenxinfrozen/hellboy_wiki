***Vampire*** is a [*B.P.R.D.*](/entry/B.P.R.D._%28series%29) flashback story cycle, telling the story of B.P.R.D. agent [Simon Anders](/entry/Simon_Anders) as he hunts down vampires. So far only the first miniseries has come out, though there are plans for more in the future.

The *Vampire* series is written by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) with the twins [Gabriel Bá](/entry/Gabriel_B%C3%A1) and [Fábio Moon](/entry/F%C3%A1bio_Moon) who also draw the comic.

## Stories Published

- *[Vampire](/entry/Vampire_%28story%29)* \#1–5 (March–July 2013)
- *[Lost Ones](/entry/Lost_Ones)* (December 2018)

## Collections

It only became apparent that *Vampire* was not a regular *B.P.R.D.* book when the trade paperback came out designating it belonging to the series "*B.P.R.D. Vampire*" instead of simply "*B.P.R.D.*". It is a subtle difference.
