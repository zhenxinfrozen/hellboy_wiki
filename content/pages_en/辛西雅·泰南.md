**Cynthia Tynan**, also known as **Cindy Tynan**, was a reporter during the 1930s for New York's *The Herald Tribune*.

Her interest in a "ghost indians" ploy by [Arnie Wald](/entry/Arnie_Wald)'s henchman [Mr. Isog](/entry/Isog) nearly got her killed. Fortunately [the Lobster](/entry/Lobster_Johnson_%28character%29) showed up when he did so she got to live on.

Cindy worked with the Lobster and his crew in stopping one of the earlier incarnations of the villain [The Black Flame](/entry/The_Black_Flame_%28character%29).  It was Cindy who came to their rescue when things looked bleak, driving a car into the Black Flame's wife [Kamala](/entry/Kamala) and killing her.

Cindy would later report the story of the Lobster's exploits against the Black Flame and subsequently reveal his existence to the public.
