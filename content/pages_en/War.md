**War** is one of the [Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse](/entry/Four_Horsemen_of_the_Apocalypse) that appeared during [Hellboy's](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) battle with [Nimue](/entry/Nimue) at the battleground of Vigrid. He led the battle against the resurrected British knight army led by the newly empowered [George Washbrook](/entry/George_Washbrook). He perished in the aftermath of the battle.
