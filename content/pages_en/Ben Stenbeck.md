**Ben Stenbeck** is a New Zealand comics artist. He made his Hellboy Universe debut in June 2008 with [*B.P.R.D.: The Ectoplasmic Man*](/entry/The_Ectoplasmic_Man). He has worked extensively with [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) on the *Baltimore* comics (not a part of the Hellboy Universe).
