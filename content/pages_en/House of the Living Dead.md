***House of the Living Dead*** is the first Hellboy [original graphic novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphic_novel).

## Publication History

The story was first published in hardcover format in November 2011. It is collected in the [*Hellboy in Mexico*](/entry/Trade_Collections) trade paperback.

## Synopsis

Devastated over the loss of his luchador comrade to [vampires](/entry/vampires), [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) lingers in Mexican bars until he's invited to participate in the ultimate wrestling match with a vicious [Frankenstein monster](/entry/Frankenstein%27s_Monster).

## Notes

- The back cover incorrectly dates this story taking place in 1952. Hellboy's time in Mexico is during 1956.
