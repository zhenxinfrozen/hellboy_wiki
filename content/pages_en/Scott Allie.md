**Scott Allie** was the editor for *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29)* and its related titles from 1994 to 2017, and also wrote several stories until 2020 when allegations of sexual assaults were leveled against him after which [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) severed all ties.

## Editor

Scott Allie stepped in as a temporary editor when [Barbara Kesel](/entry/Barbara_Kesel) stepped down as the series' editor during *[Hellboy: The Corpse](/entry/The_Corpse)*. He was told only to correct spelling mistakes, but to otherwise stay out of [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola)'s way. However, when he saw a picture where a hole looked more like a shadow, he made a note to Mignola about it. Mignola was so happy to receive this sort of feedback, he contacted Mike Richardson and informed him that he wanted Allie to remain his editor on the book.

Scott Allie was editor on all books in the Hellboy Universe (except [*Hellboy Promo* \#1](/entry/World%27s_Greatest_Paranormal_Investigator_%28SDCC%29), [*Hellboy Promo* \#2](/entry/World%27s_Greatest_Paranormal_Investigator_%28CBG%29), [*Hellboy: Seed of Destruction*](/entry/Seed_of_Destruction), [*Hellboy: The Wolves of Saint August*](/entry/The_Wolves_of_Saint_August), and the [*"Hi, My Name's Hellboy"* promo](/entry/%22Hi%2C_My_Name%27s_Hellboy%22)), until he stepped down from his position as editor October 6, 2017. His position was filled by [Katii O’Brien](/entry/Katii_O%E2%80%99Brien).

## Writer

Starting with the 2009 back-up story, *[The Burial of Katharine Baker](/entry/The_Burial_of_Katharine_Baker)*, Scott Allie began writing stories for the Hellboy Universe. He has written the following stories:

- *[The Burial of Katharine Baker](/entry/The_Burial_of_Katharine_Baker)*
- [*B.P.R.D.: The Dead Remembered*](/entry/The_Dead_Remembered)
- [*B.P.R.D.: Casualties*](/entry/Casualties)
- [*B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth—The Pickens County Horror*](/entry/The_Pickens_County_Horror)
- [*B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth—The Transformation of J.H. O'Donnell*](/entry/The_Transformation_of_J.H._O%27Donnell)
- [*B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth—The Abyss of Time*](/entry/The_Abyss_of_Time)
- [*Abe Sapien: The Calm Before the Storm*](/entry/The_Calm_Before_the_Storm)
- *[Broken Vessels](/entry/Broken_Vessels)*
- *[The Last Witch of Fairfield](/entry/The_Last_Witch_of_Fairfield)*
- [*Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.: Saturn Returns*](/entry/Saturn_Returns)
- *[The Beast of Ingelheim](/entry/The_Beast_of_Ingelheim)*
- *[Frankenstein Undone](/entry/Frankenstein_Undone)* (canceled, incomplete)
- *[The Last Knight of St. Hagan](/entry/The_Last_Knight_of_St._Hagan)* (canceled)

### *Abe Sapien* ongoing series

Scott Allie took over lead writing duties for *[Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien_%28series%29)* when it became an ongoing series in 2013. He wrote every issue since with Mike Mignola, except [*Abe Sapien: The New Race of Man*](/entry/The_New_Race_of_Man) which was written by John Arcudi and Mike Mignola.

### *B.P.R.D.: The Devil You Know*

Scott Allie co-wrote the third and final [story cycle of *B.P.R.D.*](/entry/The_Devil_You_Know_%28story_cycle%29) with Mike Mignola.
