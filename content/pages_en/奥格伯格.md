***The Ogopogo*** is a one-shot arc in May 2015 with art by Kevin Nowlan.

## Synopsis

Hellboy and Abe Sapien search for the infamous lake monster Ogopogo in British Columbia.

## Trivia

The story is referenced in the epilogue of *[Box Full of Evil](/entry/Box_Full_of_Evil)*.
