**Haborym** is one of the demon lords in [Hell](/entry/Hell).

## History

In 1947, when a young [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character))** **consumed pancakes for breakfast, most of the demons in Hell screamed in horror. When Grand-Duke of Hell [Astaroth](/entry/Astaroth) asked his servants what was going on, [Mammon](/entry/Mammon) told about the pancakes and Haborym stated that "he will never come back to us now".

## Lore

In demonology, Haborym (also known as Aym or Aim) is a Great Duke of Hell who governs over 26 legions of demons.
