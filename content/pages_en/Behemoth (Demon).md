**Behemoth** was one of the largest [demons](/entry/Demon), alongside [Leviathan](/entry/Leviathan).

## History

Both Behemoth, alongside the [Leviathan](/entry/Leviathan), were some of the largest and most powerful demons in [Hell](/entry/Hell).

After the death of [Satan](/entry/Satan) and [Astaroth](/entry/Astaroth), Behemoth and Leviathan confronted Hellboy, who decided to unleash his powers on all of demon-kind. The two biblical beasts battled the Cambion, but both were eventually defeated and killed by Hellboy after he used his hellfire powers.
