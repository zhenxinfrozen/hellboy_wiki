***Hellboy: An Assortment of Horrors*** is the fourth anthology collecting non-chronological short stories written for the [*Hellboy* novel series](/entry/Hellboy_Universe_novels), based on the comic book series by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola), who provided cover illustrations for each story.

## Contents

### *The Promised Smile*

Written by Rio Youers

Hellboy and a rookie agent Casper Morrow are sent by Cleveland Malak, the spoiled son of a Malaysian millionaire, to rescue his wife, Aidra, from a remote island, after she was abducted by a *kichiimul* (a Katamais witch) who has taken her mouth.

**Note:** This is the first of two stories in this collection that is canon.[1] The events of this story are later referenced in [*B.P.R.D.: The Devil You Know—Pandemonium* \#2](/entry/Pandemonium_%28story_arc%29).

### *When Doves Cry*

Written by Delilah S. Dawson

The NYPD asks Hellboy for help in catching a serial killer whose victims have all been ripped to pieces - as if dropped into a shredder - in their own homes.

### *The Other Side of Summer*

Written by [Chris Roberson](/entry/Chris_Roberson)

**Fairfield, Connecticut, 1950**: While browsing through a local bookshop - one of the few occasions when he is allowed outside the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense)'s new headquarters - the 7-year-old [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy) befriends a girl who shares his passion for mysteries and superhero comics, and the two team up to investigate a farm rumored to be haunted.

**Note:** This is the second of two stories in this collection that is canon.[2]
