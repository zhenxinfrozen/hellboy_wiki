***Hellboy 20th Anniversary Sampler*** is a sampler created for the 20th Anniversary celebrations on "Hellboy Day", March 22nd, 2014. It reprints the stories *[The Ghoul](/entry/The_Ghoul)* and *[Another Day at the Office](/entry/Another_Day_at_the_Office)*, and prints here for the first time *[The Coffin Man](/entry/The_Coffin_Man)*, a story with Hellboy set in Mexico, 1956 with art by [Fábio Moon](/entry/F%C3%A1bio_Moon), and a series of parody comic strips, *[B.P.R.D. Color Comics Fun!](/entry/B.P.R.D._Color_Comics_Fun%21)*, by [R. Sikoryak](/entry/R._Sikoryak).
