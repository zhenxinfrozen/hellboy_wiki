**Werewolves** are humans who are cursed to become feral bipedal humanoid wolf-like beasts in certain intervals.

## History

The royal family of [Griart](/entry/Griart) had been cursed in 1214, so that they would transform into werewolves every seven years. The villagers caught the family while changed, and killed them.

In 1967, [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) meets [Yad Tovich](/entry/Yad_Tovich), a man who specializes in the hunting of werewolves.
