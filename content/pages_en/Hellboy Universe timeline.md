## 110,000 BC

- A [Hyperborean](/entry/Hyperboreans) burial mound is pillaged.

## 14th century BC

**1338 BC**

- [Panya](/entry/Panya_(character)) overhears her father, Meryra, and another man arguing over the religious upheaval brought forward by Pharaoh Akhenaten.

## 6th century BC

**525 BC**

- Cambyses II, King of Kings of the Achaemenid Empire, sends a force of 50,000 men to conquer Egypt. In a single sandstorm, the whole army vanished in the sands.

## 1st century AD

**60**

- Boudicca is believed to have been killed by the Romans.

## 6th century

- Fall of the Kingdom of Funan. An underground cult will maintain the tradition of [the Black Flame](/entry/The_Black_Flame_(character)) until bringing about its incarnation in the 20th century.

## 10th century

**964**

- **June**: St Dunstan binds the demon [Ualac](/entry/Ualac) in a box. Legend merely records that he tweaked the devil's nose with tongs.

## 11th century

**1046**

- **September 24**
  - Saint Gerard is martyred.

**1082**

- Pope Gregory VII writes to the Bishop of Milan with the truth about Dunstan's binding of the devil.

## 13th century

During this century humans still made sacrifices to the Ogdru Jahad on an island in the center of Lake Talutah, in modern day New York.

**1214**

- The royal family of [Griart](/entry/Griart) are cursed with lycanthropy.

**1222**

- The Chutt tribe come down from Siberia to fight for Ghengis Khan, whom they believe to be the divinely-appointed emperor of the world.

**1227**

- Death of Ghengis Khan. The Chutt return to their mountain and are never heard of again.

**1291**

- [Hagan Douglas](/entry/Hagan_Douglas) is captured by Mamelukes outside the city of Acre. He is then decapitated and the body is sent into the city. On **May 28**, the Mamelukes storm the final stronghold in Acre, taking the city. Those knights that survived claimed they witness the headless body of Hagan fighting alongside them. These men would go one to the found the [Order of the Knights of Saint Hagan](/entry/Order_of_the_Knights_of_Saint_Hagan).

**1299**

- [Romulus Diovanni](/entry/Romulus_Diovanni) is burned at the stake by the [Knights Templar](/entry/Knights_Templar) in Piazza St. Marcos.

## 14th century

**1307**

- [Pope Clement V](/entry/Pope_Clement_V) recalls the Knights of Saint Hagan from the Holy Land to carry out the arrest of the Knights Templar.

## 15th century

**1412**

- The dying Johannes von Berg records his encounter with the Beast of Ingelheim.

**1418**

- Mihail Giurescu is born.

**1492**

- Mihail Giurescu finds the dried out body of [Hecate](/entry/Hecate). He restores her and builds her a temple.

## 16th century

**1513**

- [Vladimir Giurescu](/entry/Vladimir_Giurescu) takes over his father's estate after Mihail turned 107.

**1592**

- Bishop Oleksander sells his soul to [Satan](/entry/Satan) in order to gain a book filled with arcane knowledge.

**1773**

- **Unspecified Date**
  - Jean-Marie de Grigny performs an opera inspired by the events of Baron Konig's party two years prior, *Carnaval des Condamnes*. Riots ensued and de Grigny is locked in the basement of an asylum and left to die as his works are destroyed.

## 20th century

### 1900s

**1901**

- **January 22**: Queen Victoria dies.

**1902**

- Dr. Carp disappears.
- [Jim Sacks](/entry/Jim_Sacks) is born in Wabash, Indiana.

**1904**

- **February 9**: On Meishin Island, [Yumiko Daimio](/entry/Yumiko_Daimio) witnesses the horrific death of her father, at the hands of Grigori Rasputin.

**1905**

- Albert Mayhew dies.
- **October**: Zoya, pregnant with [his child](/entry/Varvara), confronts Rasputin at Prince George of Leuchtenberg's house in St Petersburg, and is contemptuously dismissed. Princess George is also present, along with Rasputin's daughters Maria and Varvara.

**1906**

- Zoya's daughter is born. She names her [Varvara](/entry/Varvara) after Rasputin's legitimate daughter.
- Lady Celia Radford dies.
- **18 April**: San Francisco is devastated by an earthquake. Some suspect the Heliopic Brotherhood of Ra of responsibility.

**1907**

- [Niels Waxman](/entry/Niels_Waxman) graduates from the Ludovicht Institute of Vienna.

**1908**

- Sir Edward Grey, accompanied by Sarah Jewell, leaves London to hunt down the remains of the HBR in Europe.
- **30 June**: 800 square miles of Siberian forest are destroyed in a mysterious explosion. The HBR are again suspected of involvement. Resulting glass closely resembles Enkeladite, implying the event involved nuclear fission.

**1909**

- [Simon Bruttenholm](/entry/Simon_Bruttenholm) and Honora Grant discover a false paranormal occurrence in Shifnal.

### 1910s

**1910**

- A wooden coal mine is built in Sweetwater county, Wyoming.
- German light cruiser SMS Emden is assigned to the East Asia Squadron based at Tsingtao.
- **April: [Time Out of Mind](/entry/Time_Out_of_Mind)**
- **May**: The Black Goddess first appears to the young [Raimund Diestel](/entry/Raimund_Diestel) at an exhibition of Babylonian antiquities in Berlin.

**1911**

- Walter Edmond Heap, while en route to India, is injured in a railway accident and subsequently eaten by a tiger.
- Dr. Carp's sister dies, leaving the house abandoned.

**1912**

- **November**: Slowly dying in poverty at Guriev, Zoya tells Yuri and [Varvara](/entry/Varvara) the truth about Varvara's father. Yuri decides to ask Rasputin for help.

**1913**

- **January**: Yuri and his family pass north of Zavodoukovsk on their journey through Siberia.
- **February**: Yuri confronts Rasputin at his house in Pokrovskoye. He is killed by Rasputin, Zoya also dies, and Varvara is possessed by [Yomyael](/entry/Yomyael).

**1914**

- **August**: SMS *Emden* is detached for service as a commerce raider in the Indian Ocean. [Raimund Diestel](/entry/Raimund_Diestel) is aboard as a petty officer.
- **September**: The Battle of the Marne. Two Irish nurses present will later go on to found the Holy Sisters of the Marne school in Savannah, Georgia.
- **9 November**: SMS *Emden* is wrecked in the Cocos Islands during a fight with HMAS *Sydney*. Diestel is ashore with a landing party, who escape capture on the schooner *Ayesha*.
- **27 November**: *Ayesha* reaches the neutral port of Padang. Diestel deserts and heads north to Siam.

**1916**

- [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm) is born.
- Graf Ling de Gothe infiltrates the [Heliopic Brotherhood of Ra](/entry/Heliopic_Brotherhood_of_Ra) under the suspicions of Henry Cyril Moore, in doing so she is captured and revealed to be a Volskak. Later she breaks free taking with her the Sword of Hyperborea and Vril crystal, crashing the zeppelin in the middle of the North Sea. 
- **Late February**: Sir Edward Grey is pulled into [Hell](/entry/Hell) by [Amdusias](/entry/Amdusias).
- **December 30**: Grigori Rasputin is supposedly assassinated.

**1917**

- Sir Albert Lindsay dies.
- The Holy Sisters of the Marne school for "lady doctors" opens in Savannah, Georgia.

**1919**

- **June 7**: [Ilsa Haupstein](/entry/Ilsa_Haupstein) is born.
- **November**: Ereshkigal appears again to Diestel in an opium den in Bangkok.
