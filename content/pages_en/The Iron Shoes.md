***The Iron Shoes*** is a short story by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola).

## Publication History

In January of 1996, when it was decided to collect *[The Corpse](/entry/The_Corpse)*, a new short story was needed to fill out the issue. The six-page story *The Iron Shoes* was created and *Hellboy: The Corpse and the Iron Shoes* was born. In August 1998, the story was collected in [*Hellboy – Volume 3: The Chained Coffin and Others*](/entry/TPB). [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) described it as not an actual story but just a weird encounter.

## Synopsis

The story opens with two Folklore experts, [Edwin D. Wolf](/entry/Edwin_D._Wolf) and [Katherine Boggs](/entry/Katherine_Boggs), who state that in general iron is potent against fairies and other supernatural creatures, but that some, like [Iron Shoes](/entry/Iron_Shoes_%28character%29), are immune and actually use iron to their advantage. In Ireland, 1961, [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) enters a ruined tower and is attacked by Iron Shoes who leaps out a window. Hellboy catches his neck with a lasso, and is pulled out of the tower. Then he drags Iron Shoes to a nearby church. As the church bell tolls, Iron Shoes crumbles to dust leaving only his shoes behind. Hellboy leaves the shoes with the church's priest. The story ends with the shoes sitting next to a statue.

## Story Chronology

The story takes place in 1961, the same year as *[The Hydra and the Lion](/entry/The_Hydra_and_the_Lion)*. When collected in chronological order for the [*Hellboy* omnibus editions](/entry/Omnibus_Editions), it was placed prior to *[The Hydra and the Lion](/entry/The_Hydra_and_the_Lion)*.

*[The Corpse](/entry/The_Corpse)* and ["World's Greatest Paranormal Investigator" (CBG)](/entry/World%27s_Greatest_Paranormal_Investigator_%28CBG%29) occurred two years earlier. In 1962, Hellboy met with [Cynthia Eden-Jones](/entry/Lady_Cynthia_Eden-Jones) shortly before she died,[1] on April 15th [Elizabeth Sherman](/entry/Elizabeth_Sherman) was born in Kansas City, Kansas,[2] and on October 3rd Hellboy killed Black Annis, the cannibal hag[3] *[The Troll-Witch](/entry/The_Troll_Witch)* takes place two years later in 1963.
