***The Witch and Her Soul*** was a story created by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) for *[The Amazing Screw-On Head and Other Curious Objects](/entry/The_Amazing_Screw-On_Head_and_Other_Curious_Objects)*.

## Plot

The Devil comes to collect the soul of a now deceased witch who made a bargain with him. Two of the witches animated puppets, Manx and Hankel, are troubled to learn that as "curios objects" created by the witch they now belong to The Devil as well. Fearful of burning in Hell the two puppets strike another deal with the devil. They agree to become "evil puppets" in exchange for The Devil allowing them to remain on earth.

## Connections to other Mignola Works

Like all the stories collected in *[The Amazing Screw-On Head and Other Curious Objects](/entry/The_Amazing_Screw-On_Head_and_Other_Curious_Objects)*, *The Witch and Her Soul* has a connection to at least one of the other stories.

### *The Amazing Screw-On Head*

- The story is set in the same location that Emperor Zombie's Evil Skull Balloon blew up over in *[The Amazing Screw-On Head](/entry/The_Amazing_Screw-On_Head)*.
