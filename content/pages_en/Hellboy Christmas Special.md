***Hellboy: Christmas Special* \#1** is a 1997 anthology collection. Opening with the full-length story *[A Christmas Underground](/entry/A_Christmas_Underground)*, the rest of the Special features suitably eerie or macabre Christmas-themed tales and illustrations that do not have direct connections to *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29)*.
