The **Cronie Troll** are a subspecies of [Troll](/entry/Troll).

## Description

They are a Troll subspecies shown in Hellboy II at the Troll Market.

## Appearance

They are stumpy gray-scaled [Trolls](/entry/Troll) that possess pointed ears.

## Known Cronie Trolls

The only known is an Unnamed Cronie Troll.
