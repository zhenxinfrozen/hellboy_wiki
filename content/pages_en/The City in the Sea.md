***The City in the Sea*** is a short prose story that was published in the magazine Weird Tales. It was written by [Christopher Golden](/entry/Christopher_Golden) from a story he came up with with [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola). Mignola also provided the cover and an illustration, both colored by [Dave Stewart](/entry/Dave_Stewart). It was later adapted into a one-shot comic, [*Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.: Professor Harvey Is Gone*](/entry/Professor_Harvey_Is_Gone).
