### Various Guest Appearances, Cameos, Homages and Parodies

- Danger Unlimited #4 (May 1994) — a two-panel Hellboy cameo.
- [*Madman Comics* \#5](/entry/Madman_Comics) (Jan 1995) — Hellboy cameos in the story (appearing on four pages in total) and on the cover.
- Shi/Cyblade: The Battle for Independents #1 (Aug 1995)
- *The Foot Soldiers* \#1 (Jan 1996) — A few destroyed statues, including ones of Hellboy and Abe Sapien, can be seen in a double page spread.
- *Body Bags* \#1 (Sep 1996) — One-page cameo by Hellboy and Abe Sapien lookalikes
- Gen 13 Volume 2 #13C (Nov 1996)
- *The Heretic* \#4 (Mar 1997) — two-panel Hellboy cameo
- Predator: Kindred #4 (Mar 1997) — A flashback panel includes a calendar with a picture of Hellboy in it.
- *Savage Dragon* #41, #51 (Sep 1997, Jul 1998).
- The Authority Volume 1 #9 (Nov 1999) — [Rasputin](/entry/Grigori_Rasputin), in his Hellboy Universe design, makes a cameo among other characters.
- Sin City: Hell and Back #7 (Jan 2000) — one-panel cameo by Hellboy in a hallucination.
- *Madman Atomic Comics* \#1 (Apr 2007) — Hellboy appears in a hallucination, calling back to his appearance in *Madman Comics* \#5.
- *Criminal Macabre / The Goon: When Freaks Collide* (Jul 2011) — Hellboy appears in the last page.
- *3-D Cowboy's Cosmic Convict Challenge(rs)* (May 2016) — Hellboy appears in this comic only available at the Challengers Comics + Conversation comic book store in Chicago. 
- *Beerkada* (Mar 2018), a comic strip where Hellboy is credited as "Anaung Belle Rama" as a spoof of an actual actress in the Philippines named Anabelle Rama.
- Die!Die!Die! #14 (Mar 2021) — An [alien](/entry/Aliens) from the Hellboy Universe can be seen in a splash page featuring lots of pop culture aliens, also can be seen closer on a panel later on.

  
It is occasionally unclear whether a story should be considered canon. As a general rule, stories reprinted in a normal Hellboy or related [trade collections](/entry/Trade_Collections) can safely be seen as within continuity, while those not reprinted, or reprinted in some other way, can be assumed outside of continuity.
