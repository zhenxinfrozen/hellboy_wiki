The **Giant Bird** is a fictional monster from the *[Hellboy: Dogs of the Night](/entry/Hellboy__Dogs_of_the_Night)* game. It is unknown if this creature has a specific name, but it is only referred to in-game as the Giant Bird.

## History

### Dogs of the Night

The Giant Bird attacked [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)), who had come to the graveyard looking for his missing colleague, [Peter Johnson](/entry/Peter_Johnson). The Giant Bird was defeated by Hellboy.
