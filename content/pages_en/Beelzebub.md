**Beelzebub** was a [demon](/entry/Demon) and Prince of [Hell](/entry/Hell).

## History

After the death of [Satan](/entry/Satan), many other high-ranking demons such as [Astaroth](/entry/Astaroth), Beelzebub, and the remaining high-ranking demons gathered at his castle.

Beelzebub proposed to call forth [Pluto](/entry/Pluto) from the pit so he may take Satan's place to re-establish order. He explained how they had never forgotten him and that he was now their only hope, in order to kill Hellboy (who had Satan) and put the rampaging slave demons back in line.

This plan was never carried out, as [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)), as the "Beast of the Apocalypse", arrived on the scene, deciding to wipe out the remaining Demon-lords. Beelzebub witnessed a Hellboy defeat both Leviathan and Behemoth (demon), and the destroyed the prince's castle with lighting.

Beelzebub and the other demon lords were then killed by countless slave demons who had followed Hellboy.
