***A Plague of Wasps*** is an original audio drama from GraphicAudio. It is a continuation of *[Hellboy in Love](/entry/Hellboy_in_Love)* and the first part of a loose trilogy of stories, followed by *[Lobster Johnson: The Proteus Club](/entry/The_Proteus_Club)* and *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.: The Goddess of Manhattan](/entry/The_Goddess_of_Manhattan)*.

## Synopsis

Hellboy has accompanied his girlfriend, Anastasia Bransfield, on a trip to Brazil to help colleagues who are conducting an archaeological dig in the jungle.

While they’re having coffee, a colleague arrives... through a public square, sick and sluggish and in agony. He falls, and wasps begin to come out of his mouth. When he bursts open, more wasps emerge—his insides are full of a labyrinthine, gray, papery nest.

It becomes clear that something sinister and supernatural is at play here.
