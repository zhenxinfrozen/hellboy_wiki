**Ratatosk** is a squirrel from Norse mythology said to run up and down the world tree, [Yggdrasil](/entry/World_Tree).

## History

### *[Hellboy: The Bones of Giants](/entry/Hellboy__The_Bones_of_Giants)*

Ratatosk appears before [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29), possessed by the spirit of [the Thunderer](/entry/Thor), and gave him warning of Thyrm and his allies. He later appeared alongside Mist, after Hellboy vanquished Thyrm.

### *[Hellboy: Darkness Calls](/entry/Darkness_Calls)*

At Yggdrasil, a squirrel worries aloud that Ragnarok has come with the death of [Vasilisa](/entry/Vasilisa), but one of the [Baba Yaga](/entry/Baba_Yaga)'s soldiers shoots him and he dies.
