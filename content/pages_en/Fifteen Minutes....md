***Fifteen Minutes...*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#8](/entry/Hellboy__Weird_Tales)*, written and drawn by [Jill Thompson](/entry/Jill_Thompson).

## Plot Synopsis

An unnamed [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) agent enlists a number of skeletons to act as set decoration for a fight that [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) has with a giant rat creature. She claims "No one want to see Hellboy fight somewhere tidy . . . they want atmosphere!"
