**Anastasia Bransfield** is an archaeologist and former lover of [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29). She was created by [Christopher Golden](/entry/Christopher_Golden) for his [*Hellboy* novel](/entry/Hellboy_Universe_novels) [*The Lost Army*](/entry/Hellboy%3A_The_Lost_Army).

Bransfield and Hellboy met and fell in love in June of 1979. Hellboy's infatuation was such that he nearly abandoned his responsibilities at the [Bureau for Paranormal Research and Defense](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense) to spend time with her, to the disapproval of his adopted father [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm). In 1981, concerned that the relationship was costing Bransfield the respect of her peers, Hellboy broke it off.[1]
