**Atlantis** was a city in the [Hyperborean](/entry/Hyperboreans) Empire.

## History

After The Hyperboean empire fell, Atlantis had sunk beneath the seas. Its people had somehow became transformed into an amphibious race known as the [merfolk](/entry/Merfolk).

Atlantis would later crumble to ruin and scraps of its glory would be collected by historians of archaeologists. So far, the city may still be inhabited by a few of the remaining merfolk.
