***The Right Hand of Doom*** is a short story that serves to recap the ongoing [*Hellboy* series](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29) for new readers while revealing new details about the [Right Hand of Doom](/entry/Right_Hand_of_Doom).

## Publication History

*The Right Hand of Doom* was published in black and white, as part of the *[Dark Horse Presents](/entry/Dark_Horse_Presents) Annual 1998*, in August 1998 (dated September). The story was reprinted in color in [*Hellboy – Volume 4: The Right Hand of Doom*](/entry/Trade_Collections). In the trade, this story was connected to *[Box Full of Evil](/entry/Box_Full_of_Evil)* by way of a new epilogue.

## Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) travels to Spain to meet with a priest Adrian Frost, son of [Malcolm Frost](/entry/Malcolm_Frost). Malcolm had been present at Hellboy's 'birth' and had spent eight years trying to have Hellboy destroyed. Adrian Frost shows Hellboy a scrap of paper written in Lemurian, with a picture of Hellboy's hand that reads "Behold The Right Hand of Doom." Adrian Frost agrees to give the text to Hellboy in return for hearing Hellboy's life story.

Hellboy recounts his 'birth' in England, childhood in New Mexico and his joining of the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) in 1952. Briefly Hellboy describes the events of *[Seed of Destruction](/entry/Seed_of_Destruction)*, *[The Chained Coffin](/entry/The_Chained_Coffin)* and *[Wake the Devil](/entry/Wake_the_Devil)* focusing on his alleged destiny. Frost asks about the Hand, and Hellboy suggests he might cut it off, but Frost retorts that it would never be safe. Hellboy leaves with the blessing of Adrian Frost, still skeptical of his 'destiny.'

## Story Chronology

*[Hellboy: The Companion](/entry/Hellboy__The_Companion)* places this story in July 1998. The year can also be determined from the story: Hellboy says that [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm) was killed four years ago, and *[Seed of Destruction](/entry/Seed_of_Destruction)* took place in 1994.

*The Right Hand of Doom* takes place between [*Abe Sapien versus Science*](/entry/Abe_Sapien_versus_Science) and [*Box Full of Evil*](/entry/Box_Full_of_Evil).
