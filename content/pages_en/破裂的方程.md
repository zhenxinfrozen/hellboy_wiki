***The Broken Equation*** is two-part arc with [Johann Kraus](/entry/Johann_Kraus) and [Agent Enos](/entry/Russell_Enos) in Japan, with art from Joe Querio.

## Synopsis

### Part One

[Johann](/entry/Johann_Kraus) travels to Japan to fight giant monsters, but what he discovers is even more disturbing. A man with no brain has been drawing pictures… and they tell a terrifying story.

### Part Two

A giant monster unlike anything the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) has seen yet battles the [Ogdru Hem](/entry/Ogdru_Hem) for dominion over Japan!
