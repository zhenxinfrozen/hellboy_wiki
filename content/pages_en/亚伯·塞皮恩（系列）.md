![](/content/images/Front_Page_-_Abe_Sapien.webp) ***Abe Sapien*** is a line of comic books about the character [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abraham_Sapien) on his solo adventures. It is a spinoff of the *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29)* series.

## Publication History

*see also [List of **Abe Sapien** stories](/entry/%3ACategory%3AAbe_Sapien_story)*

The *Abe Sapien* series made its debut with *[Drums of the Dead](/entry/Drums_of_the_Dead)* in March 1998. This first story, along with the second *Abe Sapien* story, *[Abe Sapien versus Science](/entry/Abe_Sapien_versus_Science)*, was collected in the first *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D._%28series%29)* trade paperback, *[Hollow Earth & Other Stories](/entry/TPB)*.

From *[The Drowning](/entry/The_Drowning)* onwards, *Abe Sapien* titles were released in their own trade paperback line. Stories were released sporadically with various artists until it became an ongoing series.

### [*Dark and Terrible* cycle](/entry/Dark_and_Terrible_%28story_cycle%29)

*see also [List of **Abe Sapien: Dark and Terrible** stories](/entry/%3ACategory%3AAbe_Sapien%3A_Dark_and_Terrible_story)*

Beginning with *[Dark and Terrible](/entry/Dark_and_Terrible_%28story%29)* in 2013, *Abe Sapien* became an ongoing series set parallel to the events of [*B.P.R.D. Hell on Earth*](/entry/BPRD%3F%E4%BA%BA%E9%97%B4%E5%9C%B0%E7%8B%B1)). The ongoing series *Abe Sapien* is released on a monthly basis, written by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) and [Scott Allie](/entry/Scott_Allie) with art by twin artists [Max](/entry/Max_Fiumara) and [Sebastián Fiumara](/entry/Sebasti%C3%A1n_Fiumara). The artists alternate on arcs.

To ease their schedule, the series will occasionally skip a month or do a flashback story handled by guest artists such as [Michael Avon Oeming](/entry/Michael_Avon_Oeming), [Juan Ferreyra](/entry/Juan_Ferreyra), and [Kevin Nowlan](/entry/Kevin_Nowlan). While these flashbacks are a part of the ongoing numbering, they are not a part of the *Dark and Terrible* story cycle.

## Numbering

Although *Abe Sapien* has been released as a series of miniseries and one-shots, in addition to numbers within a given miniseries, it also has an ongoing numbering system on the inside cover for the series overall. The first *Abe Sapien* story, *[Drums of the Dead](/entry/Drums_of_the_Dead)*, was unnumbered.

Beginning with *Dark and Terrible*, *Abe Sapien* became an ongoing series. In addition to the internal ongoing numbering, it now bears an ongoing numbering on the cover as as part of this shift.
