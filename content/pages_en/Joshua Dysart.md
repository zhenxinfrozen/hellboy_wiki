**Joshua Dysart** is an Eisner Award nominated American comic book writer.

## Writer

Together with [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola), Dysart worked on:

- [*Hellboy: They That Go Down to the Sea in Ships*](/entry/They_That_Go_Down_to_the_Sea_in_Ships)
- [*B.P.R.D.: 1946*](/entry/1946)
- *[Bishop Olek's Devil](/entry/Bishop_Olek%27s_Devil)*
- *[And What Shall I Find There?](/entry/And_What_Shall_I_Find_There%3F)*
- [*B.P.R.D.: 1947*](/entry/1947)
