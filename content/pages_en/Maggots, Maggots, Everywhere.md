***Maggots, Maggots, Everywhere*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy Junior Halloween Special](/entry/Hellboy_Junior)*, with both art and story by [Bill Wray](/entry/Bill_Wray).

## Plot Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) laments that all he ever gets to eat is maggots when Hitler informs him that "tasty animals" exist in another part of [Hell](/entry/Hell). Hellboy then sets off to find them but his multiple attempts to capture "tasty animals" all end in failures. Upon returning to Hitler, Hellboy discovers that a burger chain now exists in Hell but after Hitler sold him out for abandoning his post all he is allowed to order is a "McMaggot".
