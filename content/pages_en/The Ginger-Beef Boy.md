***The Ginger-Beef Boy*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy Junior Halloween Special](/entry/Hellboy_Junior)*. It was written by [Bill Wray](/entry/Bill_Wray) and drawn by [Hilary Barta](/entry/Hilary_Barta).

## Plot Synopsis

A homosexual couple get their wish of having a child granted by a naked meat fairy. The fairy causes the ginger-beef of the meal that the wife is cooking to come to life. The beef boy is initially upset that his parents were about the eat him so he runs from home. However, after encountering the realities of trying live as a piece of semi-cooked meet he returns home. His parents then force him to live in the refrigerator till winter because he has spoiled and stinks.
