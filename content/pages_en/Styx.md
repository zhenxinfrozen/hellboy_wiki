**Styx**, also called the **Stygian Sea**, is a large body of water in [Hell](/entry/Hell), which surrounds both the Lake of Fire and [Pandemonium](/entry/Pandemonium_(location)). Styx took a physical form and acted as one of Hellboy's guides to Hell, showing him the place where his mother [Sarah Hughes](/entry/Sarah_Hughes) was held and tortured until she gave birth to him.

Later after [Eligos](/entry/Eligos) was killed by [Edward Grey](/entry/Edward_Grey) who became the embodiment of the river [Acheron](/entry/Acheron_(river)), Styx, along [Phlegethon](/entry/Phlegethon), [Cocytus](/entry/Cocytus), and [Lethe](/entry/Lethe) welcomed their newly ascended brother inviting him to join them.
