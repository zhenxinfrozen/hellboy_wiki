**Bishop Zrinyi** was a clergyman that fought the Carpathian Goat.

## History

In 1767, in a little village in the Carpathian a devil arrived disguised as a goat. It caused the dead to rise from their graves and dance and crumble. This caused the villagers distress at this desecration, more so for Bishop Zrinyi who chased the demon back to [Hell](/entry/Hell) with a stick. The bishop though died a few days later and when the people laid him at his church, they found that the brass buttons on the bishop's coat had turned to pure silver and the stick he used to beat the devil into gold. The buttons were passed to a man who gave them to werewolf hunter [Yad Tovich](/entry/Yad_Tovich). Yad melted the buttons into bullets for his work, though kept three of the buttons intact which in 1967, were gievn to [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy) who used one of the buttons to fight a demon enslaved by a warlock at Goloski Station.
