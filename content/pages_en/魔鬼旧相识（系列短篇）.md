***The Devil You Know*** is the final cycle of the ongoing *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D._%28series%29)* stories, continuing on from where the [*Hell on Earth* cycle](/entry/BPRD%3A%E4%BA%BA%E9%97%B4%E5%9C%B0%E7%8B%B1)) left off.

This story cycle is co-written by [Scott Allie](/entry/Scott_Allie), following the departure of [John Arcudi](/entry/John_Arcudi). The title was formally announced on February 24, 2017, in a teaser image from Multiversity Comics.[1]

## Stories Published

- *[Messiah](/entry/Messiah)* \#1–5 (July 2017–January 2018)
- *[Pandemonium](/entry/Pandemonium_%28story_arc%29)* \#1–5 (May 2018–September 2018)
- *[Ragna Rok](/entry/Ragna_Rok_%28story_arc%29)* \#1–5 (December 2018 – April 2019)
