**Woodrow Farrier** was a cryptozoologist and [B.P.R.D.](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense) affiliate.

## History

Woodrow was interested in science and cryptozoology; however, being a black man growing up in the 1940s he was not accepted in universities and research institutes. Woodrow was only able to gain a job teaching high school math in Chicago. But one day, he was tracked down by Professor [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm), who gotten his hands on one of Woodrow's rejected research papers and recruited him to the B.P.R.D.
