**A.N. Sandhu **​was a British SOE Agent during WWII. His younger sister was Doctor [N.K. Sandhu](/entry/N.K._Sandhu).[1]

## History

Prior to meeting [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm) in France of 1941, Sandhu was a member of the Indian Imperial Police tasked with hunting the Cult of the [Black Flame](/entry/The_Black_Flame_%28character%29) in Siam.
