**Atum**, also known as **Oannes**, was a deity from Egyptian mythology and acts as a major deity to the [Heliopic Brotherhood of Ra](/entry/Heliopic_Brotherhood_of_Ra) and [Oannes Society](/entry/Oannes_Society).

The Oannes Society believe that the deity originated from the sea and would key in the future of mankind. [Langdon Everett Caul](/entry/Langdon_Everett_Caul), a member of Oannes, would go on the become the first "fishman".
