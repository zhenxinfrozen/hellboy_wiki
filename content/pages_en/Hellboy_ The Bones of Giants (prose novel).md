***Hellboy: The Bones of Giants*** is the second *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(series))* novel, based on the comic book series by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola).

## Cover Description

On the frozen shores of Sweden, lightning strikes from a clear sky. The skeleton of a huge man is revealed, its fingers clutched around the handle of an iron hammer. No one who comes to see this marvel from Norse mythology can lift it - no one but [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)), who lifts the hammer just in time for lightning to strike again, welding it to his hand and leading him towards a bizarre series of visions and encounters.

## Story Chronology

The story takes place several decades after Hellboy's first encounter with [Edmond Aickman](/entry/Edmond_Aickman) in *King Vold*. The novel includes references to [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm)'s death, which would mean it takes place after May 1994. *[Hellboy: The Companion](/entry/Hellboy__The_Companion)* places it in August 1999. However, this was changed for the [comic book adaptation](/entry/The_Bones_of_Giants_(comic_miniseries)), which moved the story to earlier in the timeline, to February 1988.
