**Lady Cynthia Eden-Jones** was a psychic and ally of Professor [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm).

## History

Lady Cynthia was formerly a famous clairvoyant in service to the British Paranormal Society, described as "Britain's top medium".

### *[Hellboy: Seed of Destruction](/entry/Seed_of_Destruction)*

Lady Cynthia Eden-Jones appeared alongside Professor Trevor Bruttenholm, [Malcolm Frost](/entry/Malcolm_Frost), [The Torch of Liberty](/entry/The_Torch_of_Liberty), and U.S. military 1st Sgt. [George Whitman](/entry/George_Whitman) during the "[Hellboy incident](/entry/Seed_of_Destruction)". She was present when a small demon suddenly appeared; contrary to Frost's reaction that it was dangerous, she considered it looked like "a little boy". Bruttenholm then followed up on this by saying "[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29)", inadvertently giving him a name.

### *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.: 1954—Ghost Moon](/entry/Ghost_Moon)*

By 1953, she was working under the [Special Intelligence Directorate](/entry/Special_Intelligence_Directorate). When one of her agents went rogue, she contacted Professor Bruttenholm to ask the B.P.R.D. to assist under the ruse that she wished to acquire a artifact in Hong Kong.

This was not their last encounter. In 1961, Hellboy met her again shortly before she died.
