**Morgan Le Fay**was [King Arthur](/entry/King_Arthur)'s half-sister, and the mother of his bastard son, Mordred.

Although Arthur's male line, and thus his name, died out, his lineage continued in a series of daughters, all witches, the last of whom was [Sarah Hughes](/entry/Sarah_Hughes), [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29)'s mother. Morgan kept Arthur's sword, [Excalibur](/entry/Excalibur), for safekeeping, waiting until Arthur's male heir was born.

Morgan lived in a castle somewhere in Europe (or in some other plane of existence) that was constantly besieged by an army of [demons](/entry/demon), including [Eligos](/entry/Eligos), demanding collection of what Morgan had promised in exchange for her sorcerous powers.[1]

During the years she spent trapped in her castle, Le Fay, along with [Queen Mab](/entry/Queen_Mab), observed the training of [Sara May Blackburn](/entry/Sara_May_Blackburn) in witchcraft at the Linton School for Girls. The school was part of a network of magical buildings created when Le Fay trapped the demon Alloces; the network acted as both a home and prison for Alloces, as well as witches he has seduced with promises of power. Alloces, who believes Sara May Blackburn is powerful enough to free him, is ultimately defeated, in part through Le Fay and Mab's machinations. [2]

After [Alice Monaghan](/entry/Alice_Monaghan) was poisoned, Hellboy brought her in desperation to Morgan's castle. Morgan cured Alice, while revealing to Hellboy the true of his ancestry and urging him to take up the sword and claim his birthright. Hellboy, reluctant, asked why she cared, since she and everyone else she had cared about were long dead. She said simply, her son should have been king, so she will settle for making Hellboy king.[3]

Later, after the conclusion of Hellboy's struggle with [Nimue](/entry/Nimue), Morgan approached [Gruagach](/entry/Gruagach), who had released the [Queen of Blood](/entry/Queen_of_Blood) from her prison, and so unwittingly caused the death of [Queen Mab](/entry/Queen_Mab) and the extinction of his own race. Although [Merlin](/entry/Merlin) had cursed Gruagach to remain in the world for eternity and see the ruin of his own people, Morgan took pity on him, lifted the curse, and took him away from the carnage on Earth. Merlin, still trapped in the grave where the Queen had imprisoned him, could only howl impotently in protest.[4]
