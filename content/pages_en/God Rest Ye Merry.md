***God Rest Ye Merry*** is short story originally appeared in the *[Hellboy Winter Special (2017)](/entry/Hellboy_Winter_Special_%282017%29)*. It focuses on [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29), [Professor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm), and "[the Visitor](/entry/the_Visitor_%28character%29)" (see *[The Visitor: How & Why He Stayed](/entry/The_Visitor__How_%26_Why_He_Stayed)*). This story is the debut of artist [Paul Grist](/entry/Paul_Grist) and colorist [Bill Crabtree](/entry/Bill_Crabtree) on a Hellboy Universe title.
