***The Astromagnet*** was a non-canonical two-page comic published by Steve Jackson Games.

## Publication History

*The Astromagnet* was published as a double-sided flyer to promote the *[Hellboy Sourcebook and Roleplaying Game](/entry/Hellboy_Sourcebook_and_Roleplaying_Game)*. It was distributed both physically and digitally.

## Plot Synopsis

[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) agent Orson Gains tracks Ubel and Arnwolf Von Braun to Rio De Janeiro in 1996. He is able to thwart their plan to cause an asteroid the size of Cuba to crash into Earth by using his jetpack as a bomb. The explosion destroys the Braun brothers' doomsday machine.
