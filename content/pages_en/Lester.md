**Lester** was a member of [Lobster Johnson](/entry/Lobster_Johnson)'s crew and served as the Lobster's sidekick, driver, and lookout.

Lester worked as a part of the Lobster's crew until he mysteriously died overseas while following up on leads concerning [Memnan Saa](/entry/Memnan_Saa).

[The Iron Prometheus](/entry/The_Iron_Prometheus) \#1 shows him to be a reader of science fiction magazine *Astounding Stories of Super-Science*.
