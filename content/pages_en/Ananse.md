**Ananse** was a trickster deity of Africa.

## History

Ananse, alongside the bats, told the witch-doctor that [Mohlomi](/entry/Mohlomi) that [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) would come to visit him.

## Lore

In Aknan folktale Anansi is the god of all knowledge of stories. He is often depicted as either a spider or a man with two pairs of arms and legs.
