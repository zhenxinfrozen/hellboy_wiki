**Frank Russell** was a American soldier and [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) field agent in 1947.

## History

Frank Russell worked in bomb and mine disposal, and served in the African Theater in the Second World War. He excelled in his duty and was offered an officer position with any intelligence organization after the war. Russell subsequently chose the B.P.R.D.

## B.P.R.D. Service

Russell, along with agents [Simon Anders](/entry/Simon_Anders), [Gabriel Ruiz](/entry/Gabriel_Ruiz) and [Jacob Stegner](/entry/Jacob_Stegner), was deployed to Chateau Lac D'Annecy, France to investigate the attacks on Nazi war criminals by the vampire [Baron Konig](/entry/Baron_Konig). After drinking at a village tavern, Anders was separated from the rest of group by an illusion cast by the spirit of vampire Katherina Brezina, who took him to the Chateau . The next morning, the three remaining operatives visited the chateau, where they discovered Anders' notebook and other personal effects. The agents then discovered two empty coffins, which bore the image of Katherina Brezina as well as her sister Annaliese. But uncovering these coffins awoke a mob of undead creatures, which promptly killed Russell along with his comrade Gabriel Ruiz.
