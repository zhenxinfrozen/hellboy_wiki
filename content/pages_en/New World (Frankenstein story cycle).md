***New World*** is a cycle of the [*Frankenstein* series](/entry/Frankenstein_(series)) set after the events of *[B.P.R.D.: The Devil You Know—Ragna Rok](/entry/Ragna_Rok_(story_arc))*.

## Stories Published

- *[New World](/entry/New_World_(Frankenstein_story_arc))* \#1–4 (August–December 2022)
- *[The Sea of Forever](/entry/The_Sea_of_Forever)* \#1–4 (February–June 2025)
- [The Speed of Darkness](/entry/The_Speed_of_Darkness) \#1-4 (April - August 2026)

## Collections

Unlike *[Frankenstein Underground](/entry/Frankenstein_Underground)*, *New World* is collected in hardcover collections.
