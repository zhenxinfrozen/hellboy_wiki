**Dwarves** or the **Nidavellim** are a race of squat humanoids from one of the nine worlds of Yggdrasil.

## Background

The Dwarves were a proud race of metalsmiths and armorers respected in the nine worlds.

## History

The Dwarves once dwelt in a separate world from Midgard, Earth. However, when the age of the gods ended, their world was one of many destroyed, thus relocated to Earth as a sanctuary. The Dwarves hid in the world of man in plain sight, in Stockholm and in Copenhagen.

After [Thor's](/entry/Thor) body was uncovered in Stockholm, its discovery revealed the location of [Thyrm's](/entry/Thrym) tankard which contained his soul. His loyal servants the Svartalves went to retrieve it, and also to kill [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) who was possessed by the spirit of the Thunderer. Eitri and Brokk however arrived and saved him, offering their services to Hellboy.

The Dwarves aided Hellboy and his allies in seeking out Thyrm and preventing the resurrection of his forces. In the end, Hellboy gifted Eitri Mjölnir, who promised that his people would safeguard it until the spirit of the Thunderer returned.
