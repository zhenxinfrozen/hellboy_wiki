**Bruno Karhu** is a German police inspector who was romantically involved with [Dr. Kate Corrigan](/entry/Kate_Corrigan). He was scheduled to meet her in England in *[An Unmarked Grave](/entry/An_Unmarked_Grave)*, but instead broke off their romantic relationship via phone from Munich.

## Triva

- *Karhu* is a Finnish surname that means "bear".
