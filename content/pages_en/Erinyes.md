The **Erinyes** or the **Furies** are supernatural entities that seek to punish those who have escaped justice.

## History

The Erinyes had attached themselves to [Hellboy's](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) soul. Two souls of doctors brought Hellboy to Dr. Hofferman to cure him. Dr. Hofferman summoned the Furies to demand why they had haunted Hellboy. It was revealed Hellboy's half-sister[ Gamori ](/entry/Gamori) had summoned them to punish them for Hellboy's part in the murder of his family. However, the Furies determined that Hellboy was innocent and turned their anger to Gamori for lying to them. Before they took her away, Hellboy asked what would happen to her. The Furies declared that Gamori would suffer at their hands, once they deemed she was punished she would be freed, left to wander to the abandoned city of [Pandemonium](/entry/Pandemonium_%28location%29) where she would witness [Satan's](/entry/Satan) corpse. She will scream in horror; her screaming will cause the city to crumble and shatter. Once Pandemonium falls the fire in [Hell](/entry/Hell) would be snuffed and it will be the end.
