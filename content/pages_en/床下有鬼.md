In ***There's Something Under My Bed***, the B.P.R.D. looks into a series of child abductions in a small town in Pennsylvania.

## Publication History

Following *[Hollow Earth](/entry/Hollow_Earth)* it was unclear what direction the *B.P.R.D.* comics would take. Four one-shot stories were published every other month from May to November 2003; *[The Soul of Venice](/entry/The_Soul_of_Venice)*, *[Dark Waters](/entry/Dark_Waters)*, *[Night Train](/entry/Night_Train)* and *There's Something Under My Bed*. At the same time during the "off" months Dark Horse published the [out of continuity](/entry/Hellboy_Comics) anthology *Hellboy: Weird Tales*.

*There's Something Under My Bed* was published in November 2003. The story was later collected in *[B.P.R.D.: The Soul of Venice & Other Stories](/entry/Trade_Collections)* in August 2004.

## Synopsis

In a small town in Pennsylvania, a little boy goes to sleep one night. A monster appeared under his bed and somehow disappeared with the child. This disappearance is the fourth in the town.

Back at the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.), they decide to look into the disappearances. [Kate Corrigan](/entry/Kate_Corrigan) sends [Elizabeth Sherman](/entry/Elizabeth_Sherman), [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien), [Roger](/entry/Roger) and [Johann Kraus](/entry/Johann_Kraus) to the home of the next potential child to try and stop the abduction. Abe hides in the girl's closet, and suddenly a monster appears there. Abe fights with the monster and stops it from taking the girl, but in the process is himself transported away.

In a run down toy store a group of monsters are arguing. The leader asks how they managed to mess up and take Abe back with them instead of the little girl. Abe, locked up with the other children, manages to stall for time. Eventually the rest of the team arrives and rescues the children.
