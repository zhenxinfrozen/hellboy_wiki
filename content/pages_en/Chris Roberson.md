**Chris Roberson** is an American science fiction author and comics writer, best known for alternate history novels, short stories, and Vertigo's *iZombie*.

## Writer

Roberson was originally hired to write *[Witchfinder: City of the Dead](/entry/City_of_the_Dead)*, but the project went so well, his role expanded greatly, becoming the primary writer for both *[Witchfinder](/entry/Witchfinder)* and *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.](/entry/Hellboy_and_the_B.P.R.D.)* as well as various one-shot series.
