***Curse of the Haunted Doily*** was a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#2](/entry/Hellboy%3A_Weird_Tales)*, with the story by [Mark Ricketts](/entry/Mark_Ricketts) and art by [Eric Wright](/entry/Eric_Wright).

## Plot Synopsis

Upon opening and accidentally getting a spot of blood on a doily owned by her recently deceased mother [Katherine Corrigan](/entry/%E5%87%AF%E7%89%B9%C2%B7%E8%80%83%E7%91%9E%E6%A0%B9)) is visited by the spirit of her overbearing, belittling mother. Kate later tells her co-workers at the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) that the only way should could get rid of the spirit was by promising to marry a doctor, move to the suburbs and have a bunch of kids.
