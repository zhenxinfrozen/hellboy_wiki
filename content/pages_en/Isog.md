Mr. Isog is [Arnie Wald](/entry/Arnie_Wald)'s main henchman, specialising in occult capers and contacts.

## Character

Mr. Isog is a regular human being, perhaps a bit on the short side. He has knowledge of and contacts in the occult spheres, hence his ploy of "[ghost indians](/entry/The_Burning_Hand)" and the hiring of [The Black Flame](/entry/The_Black_Flame_%28character%29). Mr. Isog is a calculating, cunning tactician who couches his risks in order to prosper. Nevertheless a near fatal flaw of his is that he likes to observe the playing out of his ploys closely. His acting skills allowed him to turn a capture by [Cindy Tynan](/entry/Cindy_Tynan) to his advantage before escaping.
