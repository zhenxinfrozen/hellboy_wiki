***Grind*** is one-shot arc with art from [Tyler Crook](/entry/Tyler_Crook). It is one of the most highly reviewed stories in the series.

## Synopsis

Life goes on in Santa Fe despite the [Ogdru Hem](/entry/Ogdru_Hem) on the edge of town. But the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense) comes to town too late to help at least one guy... and probably a lot more!
