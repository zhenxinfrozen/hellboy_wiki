***Professional Help*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#8](/entry/Hellboy%3A_Weird_Tales)*, written and drawn by [Evan Dorkin](/entry/Evan_Dorkin).

## Plot Synopsis

[Roger](/entry/Roger) visits with a psychologist, Dr. Ramsey, about feeling depressed. During their conversation he recounts a recent run in he had with a frost giant baby and Norwegian death metal band. He then reveals that he isn't here for psychiatric help but rather to Temhot posing as Dr. Ramsey. He manages to kill the Temhot but is left still feeling depressed following the encounter.
