**David Hyde Pierce** (b. 1959) is an American actor who has an uncredited role as the voice of [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abraham_Sapien) in the original *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(2004_film))*. Pierce is best known for the role of Niles Crane in the sit-com *Frasier*.

## Notable Roles

- Niles Crane in *Frasier* (1993-2004)
