***From Below*** is a one-shot arc of *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.](/entry/Hellboy_and_the_B.P.R.D.): 1957* written by Mike Mignola and Chris Roberson, and illustrated by Mike Norton.

## Synopsis

Hellboy, Professor Bruttenholm, and B.P.R.D. agent Archie Muraro investigate an abandoned sawmill that is rumored to be haunted. But when they get there, an unexpected discovery reveals an even stranger source for the rumors!
