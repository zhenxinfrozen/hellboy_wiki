**Sven Olafson** was an arctic explorer who lived during the 1990's.

## History

Sven Olafson escorted [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm) and three young brothers of Cavendish family into the arctic, where they found an ancient temple dedicated to the [Ogdru Jahad](/entry/Ogdru_Jahad), where they discovered [Grigori Rasputin](/entry/Grigori_Rasputin) and one of the [Ogdru Hem](/entry/Ogdru_Hem).

He alongside the three Cavendish brothers were transformed into [Frog monster](/entry/Frog_monster) by [Sadu-Hem](/entry/Sadu-Hem), and the four followed Rasputin and the eldritch entity back to America, setting base at [Cavendish Hall](/entry/Cavendish_Hall). Olafson disguised himself as the butler of the family and was later defeated by [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)).
