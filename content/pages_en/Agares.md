**Agares** was a [demon](/entry/Demon)-lord.

## History

Agares was one of the many demons who perished int he aftermath of Satan's death, alongside [Beleth](/entry/Beleth), [Shax](/entry/Shax), [Moloch](/entry/Moloch), [Amdusias](/entry/Amdusias), [Marbas](/entry/Marbas), [Orobas](/entry/Orobas), Dagon and [Rumael](/entry/Rumael). He was notably devoured by his own crocodile-like steed.

Agares' death was recounted by [Gusion](/entry/Gusion) to [Gustav Strobl](/entry/Gustav_Strobl).
