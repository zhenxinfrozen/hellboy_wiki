***The Goon* \#7** is a comic issue featuring [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) visiting the off-kilter world of [Eric Powell](/entry/Eric_Powell)'s the Goon.

A four-page framing sequence is written and drawn by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola).

## Synopsis

### Opening (Mike Mignola)

In 1965, Hellboy explores a derelict building in Lebanon, Tennessee. An old issue of *The Goon* comic magazine is nailed to the floor with an old railroad spike. Pulling it free, Hellboy notices a figure somewhat resembling his self on the cover illustration. Seeing a vision of a pipe wrench, Hellboy suddenly recoils in pain as if struck – a KRANG rings out through the empty room, though no assailant or weapon is visible.

### Story (Eric Powell)

Hellboy battles a wrench-wielding octopus on a biplane that is careening out of control, soon crashing on Lonely Street near the Goon and Franky.

Shenanigans ensue.

Tiring of his new acquaintance, the Goon strikes him with a pipe wrench.

### Closing (Mike Mignola)

Back in Tennessee, Hellboy drops the comic and spike with an exclamation of pain; the old paper magazine crumbles into dust as it lands. An annoyed Hellboy remarks "We'll just pretend *that* didn't happen."

## Trivia

- Hellboy shares the story of *[Heads](/entry/Heads)* to his newfound friends. However, that story is set in 1967 – two years *after* the frame story.
- This full issue has never been collected in *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(series))* [trade format](/entry/Trade_Collections), but elements of Mignola's artwork have been reprinted in *Hellboy*-related [art collections](/entry/Art_Books).
  - Mignola's cover art and original (inked and lettered, but uncolored) four pages of interior artwork was included in *[Hellboy: The First 20 Years](/entry/Hellboy__The_First_20_Years)*.
  - Mignola's interior artwork was reprinted in *[Mike Mignola's The Amazing Screw on Head and Other Curious Objects: Artist's Edition](/entry/The_Amazing_Screw-On_Head_and_Other_Curious_Objects)*.
  - The cover art was collected again in *[Hellboy: 25 Years of Covers](/entry/Hellboy__25_Years_of_Covers)*.
