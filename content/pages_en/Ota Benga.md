**Ota Benga** is exorcist and ex-priest who is an acquaintance of Professor [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm) and his uncle.

## History

Ota Benga is a powerful exorcist and he was called on by Professor Bruttenholm in [1947](/entry/1947) to exorcise the spirits of two [vampires](/entry/Vampire_%28creature%29) from [B.P.R.D.](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense) agent [Simon Anders](/entry/Simon_Anders). Although he was able to lock away the vampire spirits deep within Anders, he warned the Professor that this would only be a temporary measure and that Anders should be killed before the sisters break free.

When asked by Bruttenholm, Ota Benga explained he did not wish to meet the young [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy) and hinted to Trevor that keeping the young demon was dangerous and that, like agent Anders, he should kill the demon while he has the chance.

### [Exorcism](/entry/Exorcism)

Ota Benga reappears in the B.P.R.D. miniseries: *Exorcism*.  He works with present day B.P.R.D. Agent [Ashley Strode](/entry/Ashley_Strode) to exorcise the demon [Andras](/entry/Andras) from his own soul.
