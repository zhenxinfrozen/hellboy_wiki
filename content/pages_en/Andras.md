**Andras** is a [demon](/entry/Demon) and Marquis of [Hell](/entry/Hell).

## History

The demon Andras was possessing a girl in East Africa in 1890. A priest named [Ota Benga](/entry/Ota_Benga) was called in to perform an exorcism, but he decided that the demon should not return to Hell either. Instead, he imprisoned the creature in his own body.

Andras remained a demon within the body of Benga for over a century, until he was visited by [B.P.R.D.](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense)-Agent [Ashley Strode](/entry/Ashley_Strode). Strode was contacted by [Sybacco](/entry/Sybacco), a servant of Andras who wanted to release his master in order to rise the hierarchy of Hell. This demon hold the soul of a young boy hostage, threatening to take child's soul to Hell if his demands were not met.

Benga and Strode made a plan on how to kill the marquis.

Benga and Strode traveled to the Spiritual Plane, where they battled with Andras and drove him out the Benga's old body. Andras possessed a goat which was then killed by Strode.
