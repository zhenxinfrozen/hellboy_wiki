**Istra-Hem** is one of the [Ogdru Hem](/entry/Ogdru_Hem), which was trapped in the [Mona](/entry/Mona)-egg. Like [Sadu-Hem](/entry/Sadu-Hem) and the [Conquerer Worm](/entry/Conqueror_Worm_(Ogdru_Hem)), it creates [frog monsters](/entry/Frog_monster) with its breath.
