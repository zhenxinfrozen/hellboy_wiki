The *Scorched Earth* trilogy is a group of three *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D._%28series%29)* miniseries that together form a larger arc.

The three are:

- *[The Warning](/entry/The_Warning)*
- *[The Black Goddess](/entry/The_Black_Goddess)*
- *[King of Fear](/entry/King_of_Fear_%28story%29)*

Additionally, the story *[Out of Reach](/entry/Out_of_Reach)* was published as an introduction to *[The Warning](/entry/The_Warning)*, making it a prelude to the trilogy. The *Scorched Earth* trilogy serves as the conclusion to [the *Plague of Frogs* cycle](/entry/Plague_of_Frogs_cycle) and is collected in its entirety in the fourth [*B.P.R.D.: Plague of Frogs* omnibus edition](/entry/Omnibus_Editions).
