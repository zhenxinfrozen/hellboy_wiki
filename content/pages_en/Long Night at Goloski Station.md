***Long Night at Goloski Station*** is a one-shot *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.](/entry/Hellboy_and_the_B.P.R.D.)* issue, written by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) and drawn by [Matt Smith](/entry/Matt_Smith). The story was announced shortly before San Diego Comic-Con 2019. This is Smith's debut in the Hellboy Universe.

## Synopsis

A quiet night at a rundown Russian train station turns into an all-out demon battle for Hellboy when three sinister interlopers pass through.
