**Michael Allred** is a comic book artist and writer, most well-known for his series *Madman*.

## *Hellboy* Bibliography

- *[Seed of Destruction](/entry/Seed_of_Destruction)* [trade paperback](/entry/Trade_Collections) (1994) — contribution to gallery
- *[Madman Comics](/entry/Madman_Comics)* \#5 (1995) — features non-canon guest appearance from [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character))

## Trivia

- *Madman* and *Hellboy* were, for a brief time, both published under the "Legend" imprint of [Dark Horse Comics](/entry/Dark_Horse_Comics).
- Hellboy's guest appearance in *Madman Comics* \#5 is referenced in *Madman Atomic Comics* \#1 (2007), wherein Frank Einstein visualizes Hellboy when attempting to remember those he has met in his past.
  - *Madman Atomic Comics* \#3 features a brief homage to [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola)'s art, with a panel rendering characters in his recognizable style of blocky figures with heavy dramatic shadows.
