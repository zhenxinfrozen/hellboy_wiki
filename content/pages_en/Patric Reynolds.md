*Patric Reynolds* is a comic book artist. His first published work was *[And What Shall I Find There?](/entry/And_What_Shall_I_Find_There)*, originally featured on MySpace. He is the artist for [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) and [Christopher Golden](/entry/Christopher_Golden)'s comic series *Joe Golem, Occult Detective*.
