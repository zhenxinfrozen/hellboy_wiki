**Tartarus** is an area beneath [Hell](/entry/Hell) and the [Abyss](/entry/Abyss), where [Satan](/entry/Satan) and his [demons](/entry/Demon) banished [Pluto](/entry/Pluto) after having defeated the original [watchers](/entry/Watchers). Tartarus is being described as being as far below Hell as Earth is below Heaven.

## Trivia

- In Greek mythology, Tartarus is the area of the underworld where the Titans and souls of the wicked are being kept.
- Tartarus is mentioned in the New Testament (2 Peter 2:4), where fallen angels (implied to be the same ones as in the Book of Enoch) are kept due to their sins during the "days of Noah".
