**Sebastián Fiumara** is a comics artist. He made his Hellboy Universe debut in July 2013 with [*Lobster Johnson: A Scent of Lotus*](/entry/A_Scent_of_Lotus). He is one of two artists working on the [*Dark and Terrible* cycle](/entry/Dark_and_Terrible_%28story_cycle%29) of [*Abe Sapien*](/entry/Abe_Sapien_%28series%29).
