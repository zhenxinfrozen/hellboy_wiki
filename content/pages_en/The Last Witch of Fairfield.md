***The Last Witch of Fairfield*** is a short story that originally appeared in the *[Hellboy Winter Special (2017)](/entry/Hellboy_Winter_Special_%282017%29)*. It focuses on [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29), [Liz Sherman](/entry/Elizabeth_Sherman), and [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abraham_Sapien) as they investigate the disappearance of two children in Fairfield 1979.

The story is uncollected at present.
