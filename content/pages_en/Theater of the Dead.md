***Theater of the Dead*** is a non-canonical story written by [Jim Pascoe](/entry/Jim_Pascoe) and [Tom Fassbender](/entry/Tom_Fassbender) with [Simeon Wilkins](/entry/Simeon_Wilkins) providing the art.

## Publication History

*Theater of the Dead* was first published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#7](/entry/Hellboy%3A_Weird_Tales)*. The story was later collected in [*Hellboy: Weird Tales - Volume 2*](/entry/Trade_Paperback_Collections) as well as the [*Weird Tales* omnibus](/entry/Omnibus_Editions). It is also the titular story of the ''[Hellboy: The Theater of Dead and Other Stories](/entry/The_Theater_of_Dead_and_Other_Stories) collection.

## Plot Synopsis

After [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) and [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien) foil their third end-of-the-world Nazi plot in a month the two complain that they wish they could deal with something other than end-of-the-world plots. Almost immediately they discover some zombie gangsters and enthusiastically engage them; excited to be dealing with something low key for a change. Unfortunately for the two the zombies had indeed been summoned by in an end-of-the-world plot that they semi-reluctantly put a stop to.
