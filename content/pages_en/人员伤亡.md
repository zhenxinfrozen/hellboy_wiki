***Casualties*** is a short story.

## Publication History

*Casualties* was first published as a part of the Dark Horse Digital Retailer Exclusive program on July 2011, available only in the digital format. It was later collected in print as part of the trade paperback *[B.P.R.D.: Being Human](/entry/Trade_Collections)*.

This story marks the first appearance of [Agent Vaughn](/entry/Agent_Vaughn) who would later be the central character in *[The Pickens County Horror](/entry/The_Pickens_County_Horror)*.

## Synopsis

While investigating werewolf attacks, Abe makes a mistake that could have led to the death of one of his teammates.
