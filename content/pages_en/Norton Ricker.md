**Norton Ricker** was an American general who worked for the [Bureau for Paranormal Research and Defense](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense) in the 1940's.

## History

In 1947 Norton Ricker served a young [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) pancakes as breakfast, which shocked most [Demons](/entry/Demon) in [Hell](/entry/Hell).
