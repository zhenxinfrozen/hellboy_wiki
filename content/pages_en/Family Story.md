***Family Story*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#3](/entry/Hellboy__Weird_Tales)*, written by [Sara Ryan](/entry/Sara_Ryan) and drawn by [Steve Lieber](/entry/Steve_Lieber).

## Plot Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) visits a very dysfunctional family. The father, mother and son all have dealings with the supernatural. After attempting to deal with all three family members Hellboy decides to take his leave. On his way out Hellboy laments that he unfortunately killed the only decent member of the family, the cat, in a misunderstanding.
