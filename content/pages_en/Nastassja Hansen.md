**Nastassja Hansen**, also known as **Stazz**, was an agent working for the [Bureau for Paranormal Research and Defense](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense).

She, alongside [Ted Howards](/entry/Ted_Howards) and [Fenix Espejo](/entry/Fenix_Espejo), led the last team of B.P.R.D.-agents towards an undergound sanctuary where humanity could safe from the [Ogdru Hem](/entry/Ogdru_Hem). She was killed by [Frog monsters](/entry/Frog_monster) on the, though her death allowed most others to escape into the caves.
