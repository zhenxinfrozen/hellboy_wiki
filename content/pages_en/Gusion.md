**Gusion** was a [demon](/entry/Demon)-lord.

## History

Guison appeared to [Gustav Strobl](/entry/Gustav_Strobl) and told him about the death of [Satan](/entry/Satan), and the fall of [Hell](/entry/Hell)'s demonic monarchy. He notably mentions that the demon [Agares](/entry/Agares) was devoured by his own steed. Guison tried to become Strobl's new master by replacing Marbas and the new leader of the Black School, but Strobl managed to escape the demon.
