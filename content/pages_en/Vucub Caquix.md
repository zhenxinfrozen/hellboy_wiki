**Vucub Caquix** is a bird demon from Mayan mythology, which was actually an [Ogdru Hem](/entry/Ogdru_Hem)-spirit.

Vucub Caquix seeked to end the world but was confronted by the Mayan twins( *[Hunahpu and Xbalanque](/entry/Hunahpu_and_Xbalanque)* ). They battled, and though the Ogdru Hem managed to maim both brothers, they killed the creature in the end, preventing the end of the world.

## Appearance

Vucub Caquix was a large demonic bird it has red skin, sharp talons, lion tall and feather wings.

## Trivia

Vucub Caquix is inspired by Vucub Caquix.
