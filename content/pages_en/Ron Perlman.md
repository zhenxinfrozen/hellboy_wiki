**Ron Perlman** (b. 1950), is the American actor who portrays [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)). He has performed the role in the 2004 *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(2004_film))* movie and in *[Hellboy II: The Golden Army](/entry/Hellboy_II%3A_The_Golden_Army)*. He provided the voice for the character in the Hellboy animated films: *The Phantom Claw*, *[Iron Shoes](/entry/Iron_Shoes_(film))*, *Blood and Iron*, and *Sword of Storms*. He also voices Hellboy in the video game [Hellboy: The Science of Evil](/entry/Hellboy%3A_The_Science_of_Evil).

## Other Notable Roles

- The Reman Viceroy in *Star Trek: Nemesis* (2002)
- Johner in *Alien: Resurrection* (1997)
- Vincent in *Beauty and the Beast* (1987)
- Amoukar in *Quest for Fire* (1981)
- Clay in "Sons of Anarchy" (2008)
