**Cavendish Hall** was the home and seat of the Cavendish family, founded and constructed by [Elihu Cavendish](/entry/Elihu_Cavendish).

## History

Cavendish Hall was constructed on a tiny island in the center of Lake Talutah, in upstate New York, in 1841. Unknown to its founder, Elihu Cavendish, beneath Lake Talutah lay a massive underground temple where the [Ogdru Jahad](/entry/Ogdru_Jahad) had been worshiped centuries before.

In 1992, the last of the Cavendish line, the three sons of the widow Emma Cavendish, were transformed into [frog monsters](/entry/Frog_monster) due to their encounter with [Grigori Rasputin](/entry/Grigori_Rasputin) and one of the 369 [Ogdru Hem](/entry/Ogdru_Hem), called [Sadu-Hem](/entry/Sadu-Hem).  The brothers and arctic explorer [Sven Olafson](/entry/Sven_Olafson) were all transformed and enslaved to Rasputin, and he brought them back to Cavendish Hall.  [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) agents [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)), [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien), and Liz Sherman investigated Cavendish Hall and circumstances led to the destruction of the manor.
