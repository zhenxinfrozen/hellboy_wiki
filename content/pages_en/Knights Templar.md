The **Knights Templar** were a Catholic military order that was prominent during the Crusades.

## History

In 1299, the Templars burned [Romulus Diovanni](/entry/Romulus_Diovanni) at the stake in Piazza St. Marcos.

In 1307, the order was given by [Pope Clement V](/entry/Pope_Clement_V) that outlawed the Knights Templar and all members should be subsequently arrested. This order was notably carried out by the [Order of the Knights of Saint Hagan](/entry/Order_of_the_Knights_of_Saint_Hagan).
