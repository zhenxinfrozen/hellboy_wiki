**Cameron Stewart** is a comics writer and artist. He made his Hellboy Universe debut in August 2004 with [*B.P.R.D.: Another Day at the Office*](/entry/Another_Day_at_the_Office). The character [Ashley Strode](/entry/Ashley_Strode) was set aside specifically for Stewart to work with, until she was required for the *[Devil You Know](/entry/The_Devil_You_Know_%28story_cycle%29)* cycle of *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D._%28series%29)*
