**Archibald "Archie" Muraro** was a [Bureau for Paranormal Research and Defense](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense) agent.

## History

Muraro accompanied [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) on his first mission, in April (/entry/1952), and on several others during his early career.

## Trivia

- Prior to joining the Bureau in 1948, he served as a Lieutenant and B-24 pilot in the 8th Air Force.
- in 1952, Muraro took a photo of Hellboy in the ruins of [Von Klempt's](/entry/Herman_von_Klempt) Brazilian fortress. This photo was used on the front pages of newspapers and on the cover of *LIFE* magazine.
