**Matt Strackbein** is the writer and artist behind the "brief history" back-up features, which explore various characters in the Hellboy Universe. To date he's done four:

- *Kill the Black Flame* (in *B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth—End of Days* \#1)
- *Varvara: That Evil Little Russian Girl* (in *B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth—Cometh the Hour* \#2)
- *Who the Hell Is Lady Cynthia?* (in *Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.: 1954—Ghost Moon* \#2)
- *The Endless Mystery: A Brief History of Agent Howards* (in *B.P.R.D.: The Devil You Know—Messiah* \#3)

Strackbein is also a frequent guest on the Hellboy Book Club podcast.
