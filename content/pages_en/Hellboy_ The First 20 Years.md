***Hellboy: The First 20 Years*** was a book published by [Dark Horse Comics](/entry/Dark_Horse_Comics).

## Publishers Summary

*[Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) has expanded [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) into the most exciting group of books since the early Marvel universe, and his style has influenced art and illustration far beyond the world of comics. Selected finished pieces are shown alongside sketches and raw scans from the last twenty years.*
