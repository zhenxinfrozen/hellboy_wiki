This untitled story was a promotional piece for the 1994 miniseries *[Hellboy: Seed of Destruction](/entry/Seed_of_Destruction)*.

## Publication History

This four-page story was first published as a black-and-white mini-comic in *The Comic Buyer's Guide* \#1070. It was later reprinted in color in [*Hellboy – Volume 1: Seed of Destruction*](/entry/TPB), October 1994.

## Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) fights [Herman von Klempt](/entry/Herman_von_Klempt) and his "[kriegaffe](/entry/kriegaffe)" Brutus in von Klempt's laboratory. Hellboy saves the trapped woman, defeats von Klempt, and burns down the lab.

## Story Chronology

The story itself does not give us any clues as to its chronology. However, in *[Conqueror Worm](/entry/Conqueror_Worm_%28story%29)* \#2, we learn that Hellboy and von Klempt fought in Macapa, Brazil in 1959.

This is the same year as the events of *[The Corpse](/entry/The_Corpse)* (1959), one year after *[The Penanggalan](/entry/The_Penanggalan)* and *[The Crooked Man](/entry/The_Crooked_Man_%28comic_story%29)* (1958), and two years before *[The Iron Shoes](/entry/The_Iron_Shoes)* and *[The Hydra and the Lion](/entry/The_Hydra_and_the_Lion)* (1961).
