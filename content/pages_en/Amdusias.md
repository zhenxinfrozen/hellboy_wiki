**Amdusias** was a Greater Duke of [Hell](/entry/Hell).

On March 3, 1916, Amdusias was partially conjured by the [Heliopic Brotherhood of Ra](/entry/Heliopic_Brotherhood_of_Ra) in an unknown attempt to control or contain him. The summoning was interrupted by [Sir Edward Grey](/entry/Sir_Edward_Grey) who stopped the ritual, but Amdusias dragged Sir Edward to Hell. Having denied his chance to enter the mortal realm, Amdusias cursed Sir Edward with eternal life, tore his body to pieces, and then cast him into the river Acheron.

He perished at the hands of the lesser demons following the death of [Satan](/entry/Satan) and [Astaroth](/entry/Astaroth), alongside [Beelzebub](/entry/Beelzebub), [Dagon](/entry/Dagon), [Agares](/entry/Agares), [Marbas](/entry/Marbas), [Beleth](/entry/Beleth), [Orobas](/entry/Orobas), [Moloch](/entry/Moloch) and [Shax](/entry/Shax).

His corpse was later discovered by [Yomyael](/entry/Yomyael) (while possessing the body of [Varvara](/entry/Varvara)) and [Iosif Nichayko](/entry/Iosif_Nichayko) as the two arrived in Hell. Yomyael retrieved the duke's ring.
