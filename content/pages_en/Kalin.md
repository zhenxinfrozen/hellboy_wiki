**Kalin** is a fictional character from the *[Hellboy: Dogs of the Night](/entry/Hellboy%3A_Dogs_of_the_Night)* game. He is a brother of [Mitri](/entry/Mitri).

## History

### Dogs of the Night

He embraced the ancient evil of the [Nohox Kanis](/entry/Nohox_Kanis) and has allied himself with them to create a portal between Hell and Earth. His brother Mitri closed the portal nearly 700 years ago, but now he intends to do the same.

He kidnaps [Sara Courteville](/entry/Sara_Courteville) and transports her to Hell. Ultimately, Kalin is stopped and defeated by [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)).
