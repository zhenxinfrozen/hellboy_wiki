Epeke Vrooman was a Dutch warlock in the 18th century.

## Background

Epke Vrooman was a warlock that was tried in Holland in 1883. When the authorities tried to hang him, they found that the warlock used a bundle of sticks to conjure an imitation of him. In 1884 Vrooman was eventually found by [Edward Grey](/entry/Edward_Grey), living in London. With the occultist chasing him, Epke Vrooman attempted to flee by ship but Grey caught up to him about sixty miles off the west coast of France and drove one of the Lipu Daggers into Vrooman's chest. However one of Vrooman's disciples managed to use a draw a spell as the ship hit the rocks. Grey managed to escape the sinking vessel as it sank into the sea.
