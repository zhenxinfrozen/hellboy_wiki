The **Fisher of Souls** (real name unknown) was a Watcher.

He, along [Anum](/entry/Anum), [Pluto](/entry/Pluto) and the other Watchers made the dragon [Ogdru Jahad](/entry/Ogdru_Jahad), who in return became the first evil. The Watchers defeated the dragon and the [Ogdru Hem](/entry/Ogdru_Hem), and then betrayed Anum. For this treachery they were cast down into [Hell](/entry/Hell) by [God](/entry/God).

10 million years later [Satan](/entry/Satan) and his followers were cast to Hell, where they waged war against the Watchers, killing most of them. The Watcher who would become the Fisher of Souls would then be forced to built [Pandemonium](/entry/Pandemonium_(location)) and was then tasked by [Azzael](/entry/Azzael) to create an army out to the souls of the condemned. This army is meant to be commended by [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) during [Ragna Rok](/entry/Ragna_Rok_(event)).
