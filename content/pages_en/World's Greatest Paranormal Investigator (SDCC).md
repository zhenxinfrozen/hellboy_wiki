This untitled story was a promotional piece for the upcoming *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29)* series.

## Publication History

This four-page story was the first published [*Hellboy*](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29) story. It was originally published in *San Diego Comic Con Comics* \#2 (Aug '93) in black and white. It was reprinted in color in [*Hellboy – Volume 1: Seed of Destruction*](/entry/TPB), October 1994. The story was recolored by Dave Stewart for the *San Diego Comic-Con International 2018 Souvenir Book*, which commemorated the 25th Anniversary of Hellboy's first appearance.

## Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) travels to an abandoned town somewhere in the Midwest, one in a string of towns that have had all their residents disappear. Upon arrival, he notices a sign in the shape of a boot with an Egyptian scarab, and a swastika. He also encounters a lonely dog. The dog grows to immense size, and Hellboy realizes that it is [Anubis](/entry/Anubis), Egyptian god of mummification. Hellboy is able to defeat the beast, but is blown away, when Anubis lands on a flammable pump.

## Story Chronology

According to *[Hellboy: The Companion](/entry/Hellboy%3A_The_Companion)*, this story takes place in June 1993, Mesquite Creek, Arizona.
