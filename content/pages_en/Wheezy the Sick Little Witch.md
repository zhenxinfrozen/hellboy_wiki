***Wheezy the Sick Little Witch*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy Junior Halloween Special](/entry/Hellboy_Junior)*. It was written by [Bill Wray](/entry/Bill_Wray) and drawn by [Stephen DeStefano](/entry/Stephen_DeStefano). It is a parody of the Harvey Comics characters Wendy the Good Little Witch and Casper the Friendly Ghost.

## Plot Synopsis

Wheezy is sick and disobeys her mother by playing outside. She accidentally passes on her illness to a number of woodland creatures who all die horrible deaths. She then seeks out her ghost friend, Jasper, to help her. Jasper takes her inside of a sleeping giant's anus to meet a medicine troll. After Wheezy is cured the giant passes gas, expelling Wheezy and Jasper into the air. Wheezy dies on impact and then as a ghost marries Jasper. The two are shown to be living a miserable life together many years later.
