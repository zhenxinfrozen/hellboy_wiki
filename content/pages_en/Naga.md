**Naga** was a monster from Hindu and Buddhist mythology.

[Gustav Strobl](/entry/Gustav_Strobl) once lured [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) into a confrontation with a Naga in the city Angkor, whom Hellboy defeated. This confirmed to Strobl that Hellboy was the Beast of the Apocalypse and that would be on his side during the end times.
