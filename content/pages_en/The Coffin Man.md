***The Coffin Man*** is a *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28series%29)* story written by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) and drawn by [Fábio Moon](/entry/F%C3%A1bio_Moon). It was first published in *[Hellboy 20th Anniversary Sampler](/entry/Hellboy_20th_Anniversary_Sampler)*.

## Synopsis

It is set in Mexico, 1956.
