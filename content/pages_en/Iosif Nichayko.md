**Iosif Nichayko** is the current Director of Russia's [Special Sciences Service](/entry/Special_Sciences_Service).

## History

In 1948, Iosif, a Soviet soldier, volunteered for duty with Russia's [Special Sciences Service](/entry/Special_Sciences_Service). [Varvara](/entry/Varvara), Director of the SSS tasked him with protecting [Melchiorre's burgonet](/entry/Melchiorre%27s_burgonet) while it was transported by submarine. He died, along with the rest of the crew, when the submarine was sunk.

[Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien) encountered his corpse in August 1984 when he was sent to recover Melchiorre's Burgonet from the sunken submarine. Iosif seemed to be a mindless zombie, only interested in protecting the burgonet. Abe left Iosif and the burgonet in the submarine, just before the Russian authorities launched their own recovery mission.

Iosif was taken by the S.S.S. and for a while remained unresponsive. Over time, however, he began to regain awareness. He became smarter and more ambitious, moreso than he ever was in life, eventually becoming Director of the SSS after overthrowing Varvara (whom Iosif now keeps imprisoned in a glass case in his office, alongside a few relics from his previous life).

Iosif has proven himself a very capable leader, albeit a deceitful one. 

### B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth

When one of the [Ogdru Jahad](/entry/Ogdru_Jahad) arrived on Earth, Iosif reluctantly freed Varvara in gaining her help in stopping the dragon while at the cost of his life. Varvara took Iosif on a journey to [Hell](/entry/Hell) where they traveled to [Pandemonium](/entry/Pandemonium), finding [Satan's](/entry/Satan) corpse, and witnessing Varvara freeing the [Watchers](/entry/Watchers) that supported the great city of Hell.

He and Varvara watched in awe as the Watchers fought the Ogdru Jahad. As Iosif hoped that the fallen angels would defeat the dragon, Varvara told him it would be not as the Watchers do not have the power they previously possessed before they were imprisoned. Varvara then suddenly stabbed Iosif with the knife that slain Satan, shattering his soul to pieces and making him less of a ghost and unable to die.
