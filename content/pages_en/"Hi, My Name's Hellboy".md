***"Hi, My Name's Hellboy"*** is a one-page promotional strip that advertised Hellboy and his upcoming stories.

## Publication History

This page was originally published in October 1994, in "Celebrate Diversity." The page was reprinted in *[The Art of Hellboy](/entry/The_Art_of_Hellboy)* in March 2003.

## Synopsis

The one page story introduces [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29), and tells something of his origin, as revealed in *[Seed of Destruction](/entry/Seed_of_Destruction)*. The page also gives a very brief introduction of [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien). The bottom of the page is devoted to promoting the next Hellboy mini-series, *[Wake the Devil](/entry/Wake_the_Devil)*, and a group of four short stories that were to appear as back-ups to the first *Monkeyman & O'Brien* miniseries.

*"Hi, My Name's Hellboy"* advertised *Wake the Devil* as a four-issue miniseries, though it would end up as five issues. The back-ups in *Monkeyman & O'Brien* never happened but three of the stories were eventually printed elsewhere; *[Heads](/entry/Heads)*, *[The Nature of the Beast](/entry/The_Nature_of_the_Beast)* and *[The Baba Yaga](/entry/The_Baba_Yaga)*. It is possible that the fourth story, about "a possessed torture device" was meant to refer to [Elizabeth Bathory's Maiden of Joo](/entry/Hecate), which became a part of the *[Wake the Devil](/entry/Wake_the_Devil)* storyline.
