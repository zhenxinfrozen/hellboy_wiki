**John Arcudi** is an American comic book writer, best known for his work on *The Mask* and *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.)*, and his creator-owned series *Major Bummer* and *Rumble*.

## Writer

Arcudi's first *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D._%28series%29)* work was the short story *[Born Again](/entry/Born_Again)*, published as part of *Hellboy Premiere Edition* in March 2004. Arcudi has served as writer for a large number of *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D._%28series%29)* stories, as well as a number of other related titles, almost always with [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola) as co-writer.
