***Secret Nature*** is a one-shot arc of *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.](/entry/Hellboy_and_the_B.P.R.D.): 1955* written by Mike Mignola and Chris Roberson, and illustrated by Shawn Martinbrough.

## Synopsis

A missing group of teens in Oregon’s backwoods leads [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) and [Woody](/entry/Woodrow_Farrier) to confront a cryptozoological horror with origins similar to his own!
