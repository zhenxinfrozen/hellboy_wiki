***Subconscious*** is a short story written by [John Arcudi](/entry/John_Arcudi) with art by [Mark Nelson](/entry/Mark_Nelson) in his Hellboy Universe debut. It is a sequel to the 1995 story *[The Chained Coffin](/entry/The_Chained_Coffin)* and one of the few stories without a writer credit for [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola).

## Synopsis

Abe Sapien replies to a letter from Hellboy about his dreams with a letter about his own dreams.
