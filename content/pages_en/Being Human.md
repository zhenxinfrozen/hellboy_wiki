***Being Human*** is a flashback story to a mission featuring [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) and [Roger](/entry/Roger).

## Publication History

The one-shot issue was published in May 2011. Despite the original issue being a *Hellboy* title, the story was collected in the trade paperback *[B.P.R.D.: Being Human](/entry/Trade_Collections)* in November 2011. It was later recollected in the [*Hellboy – Volume 2: Strange Places* omnibus](/entry/Omnibus_Editions).

## Synopsis

A horrible witch and her zombie servant host a dinner party for a family of corpses, and Hellboy and Roger turn up to blast them all back to hell in this team-up story from Roger’s early days at the B.P.R.D.

## Story Chronology

The exact date is unclear. It must take place after *[Box Full of Evil](/entry/Box_Full_of_Evil)* but before [*Conqueror Worm*](/entry/Conqueror_Worm_%28story%29) in 2000.
