***The Garden (III)*** is a one-shot story with Max Fiumara on art.

## Synopsis

The necromancer [Gustav Strobl](/entry/Gustav_Strobl) demonstrates his new-found power as [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abraham_Sapien) reaches England in the final calm before the storm of [*Dark and Terrible*](/entry/Dark_and_Terrible_%28story_cycle%29).
