***Hellboy Jr. vs Hitler*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy Junior](/entry/Hellboy_Junior)* It was written by and drawn by [Bill Wray](/entry/Bill_Wray).

## Plot Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)) is tasked with transporting the ever abstinent [Adolf Hitler](/entry/Adolf_Hitler) to Lower Hell so that Hitler may receive his "just reward". Hellboy is given the Spear of Destiny to help keep Hitler in line and is informed that his butt is on the line if he fails to deliver Hitler on time. The pair encounter a number of set back on their journey. They constantly bicker and Hitler makes multiple escape attempts. Upon arriving at their destination Hilter is given an award for "Best Genocide" and as a reward he is crucified and stabbed with the Spear of Destiny for the rest of eternity.
