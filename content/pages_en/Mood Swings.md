***Mood Swings*** is a short story that originally appeared in the *[Hellboy Winter Special (2016)](/entry/Hellboy_Winter_Special_%282016%29)*. It is the Hellboy Universe debut of Chelsea Cain. The story focuses on [a young Liz Sherman](/entry/Elizabeth_Sherman), [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy), and [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor_Bruttenholm) on Christmas Eve, 1975.

The story remains uncollected thus far.
