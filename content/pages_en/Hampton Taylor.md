Major **​Hampton Taylor** ​was a major in the US Air Force.

## ​History

Hampton was a B-24 pilot in the Eighth Air Force during World War II, flying with his co-pilot, [Archie Muraro](/entry/Archibald_Muraro). During a [BPRD](/entry/Bureau_for_Paranormal_Research_and_Defense) investigation at Reynolds Air Force Base in the Marshall Islands during 1955, Hampton mentions to [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29), [Woodrow Farrier](/entry/Woodrow_Farrier), [Jacob Stegner](/entry/Jacob_Stegner), and Archie of the strange occurrences that happened over the week and of the E-Bomb.
