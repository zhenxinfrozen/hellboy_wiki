**Seth MacFarlane** (b. 1973), is an American writer, producer and actor who voices [Johann Kraus](/entry/Johann_Kraus) in *[Hellboy II: The Golden Army](/entry/Hellboy_II%3A_The_Golden_Army)*.

## Notable Roles

- Stan Smith in *American Dad* (2005-present)
- Peter Griffin in *Family Guy* (1999-present)
- Stewie Griffin in *Family Guy*(1999-present)
- Brian Griffin in Family Guy(1999-present)
