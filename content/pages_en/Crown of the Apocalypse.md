The Crown of the Apocalypse is a demonic artifact which manifests over [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character))'s head repeatedly, usually while his horns are regrowing (for example, when [Hecate](/entry/Hecate) transported him to the [Ogdru Jahad](/entry/Ogdru_Jahad)'s prison). After [Igor Bromhead](/entry/Igor_Bromhead) bound Hellboy, the demon [Ualac](/entry/Ualac) stole the crown and wore it, temporarily becoming the Beast of the Apocalypse. Hellboy defeated Ualac and turned him over to [Astaroth](/entry/Astaroth), who said he would keep the crown in Hell until Hellboy came there to take up his father's ring and sword.

The crown left Astaroth's possession while Hellboy was fighting a group of giants. Hellboy went on a violent rampage and slaughtered them all viciously, causing the crown to reappear over his head. It vanished after he broke off his horns, leaving it's current whereabouts unclear.
