The **Fragglewump** is a type of [troll](/entry/Troll) from Scotland featured in *[Hellboy II: The Golden Army](/entry/Hellboy_II%3A_The_Golden_Army)*. They are grey, twisted creatures with long spindly limbs, though using their glamour, they are able to hide their appearance. They have an intense fear of canaries.

While searching for the [Troll Market](/entry/The_Troll_Market), [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_(character)), [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe_Sapien), and [Johann Kraus](/entry/Johann_Kraus) found a Fragglewump Cat Seller and confronted her before she could devour a cat. By threatening it with a canary, they were able to force it to lead them to the Troll Market. When it refused to tell them how to gain entrance, even when threatened with a canary, Hellboy punched the creature, sending it into a wall.
