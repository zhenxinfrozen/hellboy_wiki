**Eric Powell** is a comic book artist and writer best known for his creator owned comic *The Goon* published by [Dark Horse Comics](/entry/Dark_Horse_Comics).

Powell contributed the [Weird Tale](/entry/Hellboy__Weird_Tales) *[Midnight Cowboy](/entry/Midnight_Cowboy)* to the second issue of *Weird Tales*. Powell also illustrated a cover for [*Hellboy and the B.P.R.D: 1954* \#1](/entry/1952) that was featured in *[The Hellboy 100 Project](/entry/The_Hellboy_100_Project).*

[*The Goon* \#7](/entry/The_Goon_7) featured [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy_%28character%29) entering the world of the Goon, with a framing sequence and cover by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike_Mignola). At the end of the story, an annoyed Hellboy remarks "we'll just pretend *that* didn't happen," implying it is not meant to be interpreted as canonical.
