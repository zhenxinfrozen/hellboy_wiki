*The Devil You Know* is the final cycle of the ongoing *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.%20%28series%29)* stories, continuing on from where the [*Hell on Earth* cycle](/entry/Hell_on_Earth_(story_cycle))) left off.

This story cycle is co-written by [Scott Allie](/entry/Scott%20Allie), following the departure of [John Arcudi](/entry/John%20Arcudi). The title was formally announced on February 24, 2017, in a teaser image from Multiversity Comics.[1]

## Stories Published

- *[Messiah](/entry/Messiah)* \#1–5 (July–December 2017)
- *[Pandemonium](/entry/Pandemonium%20%28story%20arc%29)* \#1–5 (May–September 2018)
