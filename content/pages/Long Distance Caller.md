***Long Distance Caller*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#7](/entry/Hellboy%3A%20Weird%20Tales)*, written by and drawn by [Kev Walker](/entry/Kev%20Walker).

## Plot Synopsis

[Johann Kraus](/entry/Johann%20Kraus) spends his nights floating through the atmosphere while all his co-workers at the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) sleep. One evening while floating among the clouds he is almost vaporized by the emissions of of a giant terrestrial parabolic transmitter. Choosing to investigate the situation he discovers that an malevolent extraterrestrial entity is present at the base of the dish. Kraus is able to pass an idea to the lone human still alive and with his help she is able to defeat the extraterrestrial. Upon returning to his containment suit Kraus is greeted by [Katherine Corrigan](/entry/Katherine_Corrigan)) who informs him that they are headed to Australia to investigate an incident at a S.E.T.I. facility.
