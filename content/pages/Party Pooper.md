***Party Pooper*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#1](/entry/Hellboy__Weird_Tales)*, with both art and story by [Andi Watson](/entry/Andi%20Watson).

## Plot Synopsis

[Katherine Corrigan](/entry/Katherine_Corrigan)) takes [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28character%29) out for the day while [Elizabeth Sherman](/entry/Elizabeth%20Sherman) and [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe%20Sapien) prepare a surprise birthday party for him back at [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) headquarters; complete with a pile of pancakes toped with a candle. Little does everyone else realize that Hellboy is struggling to come to terms with his birth and his fate.
