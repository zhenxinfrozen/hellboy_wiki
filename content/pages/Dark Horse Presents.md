Dark Horse Presents was an anthology title published by Dark Horse. The title ran for 157 issues, with a number of specials and annuals, between July 1986 and September 2000.

A number of issues contained Hellboy stories including;

- *Dark Horse Presents* \#88 (Aug '94) - "[The Wolves of Saint August](/entry/The%20Wolves%20of%20Saint%20August)" 1
- *Dark Horse Presents* \#89 (Sep '94) - "[The Wolves of Saint August](/entry/The%20Wolves%20of%20Saint%20August)" 2
- *Dark Horse Presents* \#90 (Oct '94) - "[The Wolves of Saint August](/entry/The%20Wolves%20of%20Saint%20August)" 3
- *Dark Horse Presents* \#91 (Nov '94) - "[The Wolves of Saint August](/entry/The%20Wolves%20of%20Saint%20August)" 4
- *Dark Horse Presents* \#100-0[1] (Jul '95) - a 3 page teaser of "[The Chained Coffin](/entry/The%20Chained%20Coffin)"
- *Dark Horse Presents* \#100-2 (Aug '95) - "[The Chained Coffin](/entry/The%20Chained%20Coffin)"
- *Dark Horse Presents Annual 1998* (Sep '98) - "[The Right Hand of Doom](/entry/The%20Right%20Hand%20of%20Doom)"
- *Dark Horse Presents Annual 1999* (Aug '99) - "[Pancakes](/entry/Pancakes)"
- *Dark Horse Presents* \#151 (Feb '00) - "[The Nature of the Beast](/entry/The%20Nature%20of%20the%20Beast)"
