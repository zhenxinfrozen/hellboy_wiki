**Gall Dennar** was a prehistoric human and tribe leader.

## History

Gall Dennar was a tribal leader in prehistoric times following the fall of the Hyperboreans.

His broken, Hyperborean blade by his grandfather and was able to turn the blade into a powerful weapon. The sword was then passed on to Gall's father, and then to himself.

As time went on, Gall continued to use his blade to fight clans of Cold People.
