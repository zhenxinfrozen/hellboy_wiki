**A.N. Sandhu **​was a Britisih SOE Agent during WWII.

## History

Prior to meeting [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor%20Bruttenholm) in France of 1941, Sandhu was a member of the Indian Imperial Police tasked with hunting the Cult of the Black Flame in Siam.
