**Lord Michael Glaren** was a high ranking member of the [Heliopic Brotherhood of Ra](/entry/Heliopic%20Brotherhood%20of%20Ra). He is notable for his association with [Panya](/entry/Panya) during her time spent living in Victorian society. He is the younger brother of [Lord Adam Glaren](/entry/Adam%20Glaren).
