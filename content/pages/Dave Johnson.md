**Dave Johnson** is an Eisner-winning comics artist (2002, *100 Bullets*). He did covers for [*Abe Sapien*](/entry/Abe%20Sapien%20%28series%29), [*Lobster Johnson*](/entry/Lobster%20Johnson%20%28series%29) and [*B.P.R.D.*](/entry/B.P.R.D.%20%28series%29) from October 2009 to August 2013.
