*Patric Reynolds* is a comic book artist. His first published work was *[And What Shall I Find There?](../pages/And What Shall I Find There?.md)*, originally featured on MySpace. He is the artist for [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike%20Mignola) and [Christopher Golden](/entry/Christopher%20Golden)'s comic series *Joe Golem, Occult Detective*.
