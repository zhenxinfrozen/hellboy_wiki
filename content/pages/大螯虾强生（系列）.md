***Lobster Johnson*** is a line of comic books about the character [The Lobster](/entry/Lobster%20Johnson%20%28character%29). It is a spin-off of the *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28series%29)* series.

## Publication History

The *Lobster Johnson* series made its debut with a short backup story, *[The Killer in My Skull](/entry/The%20Killer%20in%20My%20Skull)*, in [*Hellboy: Box Full of Evil* \#1](/entry/Box%20Full%20of%20Evil). This story would later be collected in the [first volume of the B.P.R.D. trades](/entry/TPB).

In September 2007, the *Lobster Johnson* debuted its first full miniseries, *[The Iron Prometheus](/entry/The%20Iron%20Prometheus)*, written by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike%20Mignola) with art by [Jason Armstrong](/entry/Jason%20Armstrong). Set in 1937, this story explored [Memnan Saa](/entry/Memnan%20Saa), setting up plot elements for *[B.P.R.D.: The Black Goddess](/entry/The%20Black%20Goddess)*.

In January 2012, *Lobster Johnson* returned for *[The Burning Hand](/entry/The%20Burning%20Hand)*, a five-issue miniseries that takes place in 1932 at the beginning of the Lobster's career. [John Arcudi](/entry/John%20Arcudi) became the ongoing writer for the series with this title, saying that future *Lobster Johnson* stories would continue chronologically from that point. This is mostly true, although the one-shot stories often jump back and forth as much as a year. However, they are collected chronologically in the trade paperback collections.

*The Burning Hand* also saw [Tonci Zonjic](/entry/Tonci%20Zonjic) join *[Lobster Johnson](/entry/Lobster%20Johnson)* as the primary artist on the series. He draws most of the longer arcs, while guest artists usually draw the one-shot stories.

A full list of *Lobster Johnson* stories can be found here: List of **Lobster Johnson** stories

## Collections

### Trade Paperbacks

  
*see also [Lobster Johnson Trade Paperback Collections](/entry/TPB)*  

All in-continuity *Lobster Johnson* stories are collected into trade paperbacks eventually. So far there have been four volumes. A fifth and a sixth will likely come out in late 2017. The standard trades usually contain a sketchbook or gallery.
