**Duncan Fegredo** is a British comic book artist. He was the ongoing *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28series%29)* artist from 2007 to 2011.

## Interior Artist

Duncan Fegredo began working in the Hellboy Universe when he took over the duties of ongoing *Hellboy* artist from [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike%20Mignola) beginning with *[Darkness Calls](/entry/Darkness%20Calls)*. He stepped down as ongoing artist when he finished *[The Fury](/entry/The%20Fury)*, but has remained a part of the Hellboy Universe team.
