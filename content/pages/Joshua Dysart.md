**Joshua Dysart** is an Eisner Award nominated American comic book writer.

## Writer

Together with [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike%20Mignola), Dysart worked on:

- [*Hellboy: They That Go Down to the Sea in Ships*](/entry/They%20That%20Go%20Down%20to%20the%20Sea%20in%20Ships)
- [*B.P.R.D.: 1946*](/entry/1946)
- *[Bishop Olek's Devil](/entry/Bishop%20Olek%27s%20Devil)*
- *[And What Shall I Find There?](../pages/And What Shall I Find There?.md)*
- [*B.P.R.D.: 1947*](/entry/1947)
