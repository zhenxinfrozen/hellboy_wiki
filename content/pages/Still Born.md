***Still Born*** was a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#3](/entry/Hellboy%3A%20Weird%20Tales)*, written by [Matt Hollingsworth](/entry/Matt%20Hollingsworth) and [Alex Maleev](/entry/Alex%20Maleev) and, art by [Alex Maleev](/entry/Alex%20Maleev).

## Plot Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28character%29), [Elizabeth Sherman](/entry/Elizabeth%20Sherman) and [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe%20Sapien) visit a hospital where they believe a potentially dangerous baby is going to be born. While Sherman and Sapien attempt to negotiate with a doctor in order to be present in the delivery room Hellboy has a vision that leads him to believe that the baby won't be born just as an announcement comes over the hospital's public address alerting doctors to a mother in cardiac arrest.
