盖·戴维斯

黑暗水域，万能机械，BPRD系列的主画师。 画风粗狂，构图标准严谨，分镜精彩。线条偏向圆润和细节纹理勾勒。没有mike的刚硬的钢笔转折线条，更多的像是软铅的柔和。，人物脸型感觉像是方土豆。很多鞋拔子脸。

**Guy Davis** is an American comic book artist primarily known for his work on *Sandman Mystery Theatre* and *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.%20%28series%29)*. He made his debut in the Hellboy Universe with [*B.P.R.D.: Dark Waters*](/entry/Dark%20Waters) in July 2003. He was the regular artist for *B.P.R.D.* from March 2004 to March 2011.
