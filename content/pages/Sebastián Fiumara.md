**Sebastián Fiumara** is a comics artist. He made his Hellboy Universe debut in July 2013 with [*Lobster Johnson: A Scent of Lotus*](/entry/A%20Scent%20of%20Lotus). He is one of two artists working on the [*Dark and Terrible* cycle](/entry/Dark%20and%20Terrible%20%28story%20cycle%29) of [*Abe Sapien*](/entry/Abe%20Sapien%20%28series%29).
