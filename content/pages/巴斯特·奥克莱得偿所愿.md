***Buster Oakley Gets His Wish*** is a *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28series%29)* one-shot written by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike%20Mignola) with art by [Kevin Nowlan](/entry/Kevin%20Nowlan).

## Synopsis

Sucked into a giant spacecraft full of mutated livestock, Hellboy must fight his way through giant robots, strange webbing, and evil aliens to get back to terra firma!
