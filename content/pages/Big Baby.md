"**Big Baby**" is the name [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28character%29) gives to his gun in *[Hellboy II: The Golden Army](/entry/Hellboy_II__The_Golden_Army)*.

## Weapon specification

The Big Baby is an oversized, six-round gun. It contains small rockets which causes a small explosion. Due to its size, Hellboy has to carry the weapon with both hands. But in the Hellboy II film, Hellboy can hold it with the Right Hand of Doom. It was used against a Forest God. As it is shown in the Hellboy II film, it fires two rockets at a time.

## Rounds

The only known rounds are small rockets with the words "suck on this" on the side of the cartridges.

## Other Weapons

In the second volume of the *Hellboy* series he used a large semi-automatic pistol (developed by the sinister Zinco company) that quickly explodes in his hand, presumably due to sabotage. In "The Wolves of St. August" he is seen carrying a bizarre looking gun of unknown specifications. He also has a Good Samaritan as his main weapon.
