**Frank Russell** was a American soldier and [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) field agent in 1947.

## History

Frank Russell worked in bomb and mine disposal, and served in the African Theater in the Second World War. He excelled his duty and was offered an officer position with any intelligence organization after the war. Russell subsequently chose the B.P.R.D.
