***Theater of the Dead*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#7](/entry/Hellboy__Weird_Tales)*, written by [Jim Pascoe](/entry/Jim%20Pascoe) and [Tom Fassbender](/entry/Tom%20Fassbender) with [Simeon Wilkins](/entry/Simeon%20Wilkins) providing the art.

## Plot Synopsis

After [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28character%29) and [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe%20Sapien) foil their third end-of-the-world Nazi plot in a month the two complain that they wish they could deal with something other than end-of-the-world plots. Almost immediately they discover some zombie gangsters and enthusiastically engage them; excited to be dealing with something low key for a change. Unfortunately for the two the zombies had indeed been summoned by in an end-of-the-world plot that they semi-reluctantly put a stop to.
