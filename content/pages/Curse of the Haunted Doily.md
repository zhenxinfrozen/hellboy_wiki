***Curse of the Haunted Doily*** was a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#2](/entry/Hellboy%3A%20Weird%20Tales)*, with the story by [Mark Ricketts](/entry/Mark%20Ricketts) and art by Eric Wright.

## Plot Synopsis

Upon opening and accidentally getting a spot of blood on a doily owned by her recently deceased mother [Katherine Corrigan](/entry/Katherine_Corrigan)) is visited by the spirit of her overbearing, belittling mother. Kate later tells her co-workers at the [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) that the only way should could get rid of the spirit was by promising to marry a doctor, move to the suburbs and have a bunch of kids.
