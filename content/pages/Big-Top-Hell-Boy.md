***Big-Top-Hell-Boy*** was a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#1](/entry/Hellboy__Weird_Tales)*, with both art and story by [John Cassaday](/entry/John%20Cassaday).

## Plot Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28character%29) meets up with [Elizabeth Sherman](/entry/Elizabeth%20Sherman) and [Abe Sapien](/entry/Abe%20Sapien) in Marktleuthen, Germany in 1994 where a ghost circus has appeared and 13 children have gone missing. Hellboy is able to use his [Right Hand of Doom](/entry/Right%20Hand%20of%20Doom) in conjunction with Sherman's pyrokinetic abilities to activate a spector-bomb and put an end to the ghost circus.
