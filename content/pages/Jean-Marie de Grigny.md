**Jean-Marie de Grigny** was a 18th century French composer.

## History

In the summer of 1771, Jean-Marie de Grigny attended a party hosted by the vampire [Baron Konig](/entry/Baron%20Konig) at Lac d'Annecy. Two years later, de Grigny created an opera he claimed was inspired by Konig's party. The work offended its audience to such an extent that a mob burned down the opera house on opening night.
