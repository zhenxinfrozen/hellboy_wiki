**Lester** was a member of [Lobster Johnson](/entry/Lobster%20Johnson)'s crew and served as the Lobster's sidekick, driver, and lookout.

Lester worked as a part of the Lobster's crew until he mysteriously died overseas while following up on leads concerning [Memnan Saa](/entry/Memnan%20Saa).
