**Fábio Moon** is an Brazilian comics writer and artist. He made his Hellboy Universe debut in July 2009 with [*B.P.R.D.: 1947*](/entry/1947).

## Writer

Fábio Moon co-wrote [*B.P.R.D.: Vampire*](/entry/Vampire%20%28story%29) with [Gabriel Bá](/entry/Gabriel%20B%C3%A1) and Mike Mignola.
