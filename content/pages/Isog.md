Mr. Isog is Arnie Wald's main henchman, specialising in occult capers and contacts.

## Character

Mr. Isog is a regular human being, perhaps a bit on the short side. He has knowledge of and contacts in the occult spheres, hence his ploy of "[ghost indians](/entry/The%20Burning%20Hand)" and the hiring of [The Black Flame](/entry/The%20Black%20Flame%20%28character%29). Mr. Isog is a calculating, cunning tactician who couches his risks in order to prosper. Nevertheless a near fatal flaw of his is that he likes to observe the playing out of his ploys closely. His acting skills allowed him to turn a capture by Cindy Tynan to his advantage before escaping.
