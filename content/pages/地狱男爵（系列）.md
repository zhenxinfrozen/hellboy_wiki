***Hellboy*** is a line of comic books about the character [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28character%29).

## Publication History

The *Hellboy* series made its [first appearance](/entry/World%27s%20Greatest%20Paranormal%20Investigator%20%28SDCC%29) as a four-page, black and white comic by Mike Mignola with a script by John Byrne published by Dark Horse Comics in San Diego Comic-Con Comics \#2 (19 August 1993), distributed at the San Diego Comic-Con. This piece was used to promotional piece for Hellboy's debut miniseries *[Seed of Destruction](/entry/Seed%20of%20Destruction)*.

After the conclusion of *Seed of Destruction*, Mike Mignola took over scripting duties on the comics and has since written every *Hellboy* story himself, with the exception of *[They That Go Down to the Sea in Ships](/entry/They%20That%20Go%20Down%20to%20the%20Sea%20in%20Ships)* which he co-wrote with [Joshua Dysart](/entry/Joshua%20Dysart).

The series is usually released as a series of one-shots or miniseries, some of these advancing the ongoing storyline, others being more stand-alone tales, although there are also numerous short stories that have been published in a wide variety of publications, including digital publications.

Mike Mignola was the sole artist on *Hellboy* until *[Makoma](/entry/Makoma)* in February 2006, drawn by [Richard Corben](/entry/Richard%20Corben) with a framing sequence by Mike Mignola. Since then, a *Hellboy* story with Mike Mignola as artist has become somewhat of a rarity, usually with a single Mignola-drawn issue coming out over a two year period. However, the output has increased, as Mignola has invited other artists to work on his comic including [P. Craig Russell](/entry/P.%20Craig%20Russell), [Scott Hampton](/entry/Scott%20Hampton), [Kevin Nowlan](/entry/Kevin%20Nowlan), [Jason Shawn Alexander](/entry/Jason%20Shawn%20Alexander) and [Duncan Fegredo](/entry/Duncan%20Fegredo), who took over the ongoing storyline for the miniseries *[Darkness Calls](/entry/Darkness%20Calls) until* *[The Fury](/entry/The%20Fury)*.

November 2011 saw the publication of the first *Hellboy* original graphic novel, *[House of the Living Dead](/entry/House%20of%20the%20Living%20Dead)*. This story was roughly two-issues of content released as a hard cover and was colllected along with related stories in [*Hellboy in Mexico*](/entry/Hellboy%20in%20Mexico). In 2013 a second graphic novel was released, [*The Midnight Circus*](/entry/The%20Midnight%20Circus), which has remained uncollected in the trade paperback form. [*Into the Silent Sea*](/entry/Into%20the%20Silent%20Sea), with art by [Gary Gianni](/entry/Gary%20Gianni), is another story in this format which is scheduled for release in April 2017.

Beginning December 2012, Mike Mignola will return as the ongoing artist for *[Hellboy in Hell](/entry/Hellboy%20in%20Hell%20%28series%29)*. *Hellboy in Hell* marks a departure for the comic series, since it is not a miniseries or one-shot, but an ongoing series. Despite plans for this series to continue into four trade paperbacks, Mignola decided to cut the series short with the tenth issue, [*For Whom the Bell Tolls*](/entry/For%20Whom%20the%20Bell%20Tolls).

## Types of Stories

The stories can generally be split into two broad categories:

- **Pre-Cavendish** (can be read at any time)
- **Post-Cavendish** (recommended to be read in order)

*Cavendish* refers to the events of *[Seed of Destruction](/entry/Seed%20of%20Destruction)*, which mostly focused on the *Cavendish Hall Incident* of May 1994. Pre-Cavendish stories tend to be smaller, stand-alone stories and are published out of chronological order, usually as shorts or one-shots, occasionally doing a full mini-series. Post-Cavendish stories focus prominently on the ongoing *Hellboy* story and have a clear chronological progression. These stories are usually miniseries.

The series has also been divided into chapters by Mike Mignola.

  
**The First Chapter of Hellboy's story** covers the internal continuity dating from Hellboy's birth to his death and resurrection in *[The Island](/entry/The%20Island)*.[1]

**The Middle Chapter of Hellboy's story** covers the internal continuity from Hellboy leaving for Britain at the end of *[The Island](/entry/The%20Island)* to the events of *[The Fury](/entry/The%20Fury)*.[2]

**The Final Chapter of Hellboy's story** will cover the ongoing story in *[Hellboy in Hell](/entry/Hellboy%20in%20Hell%20%28series%29)*.

## Collections

### Trade Paperbacks

  
*see also [Hellboy Trade Paperback Collections](/entry/TPB)*  

All in-continuity *Hellboy* stories are collected into trade paperbacks eventually. So far there has been twelve volumes, with additional volumes for out-of-continuity stories such as the *Weird Tales* stories and cross-overs. The standard trades usually contain a sketchbook or gallery and most stories come with story notes by Mike Mignola (*Hellboy: The Storm and the Fury* was a rare exception). *Hellboy in Hell* is collected as its own line of trades.

### Library Editions

  
*see also [Hellboy Library Editions](/entry/Hellboy%20Library%20Editions)  
*

Beginning May 2008, Dark Horse decided to release special hard cover editions of *Hellboy* reproducing the artwork at its original size. These large volumes contain the material from two of the standard trades, plus a greatly expanded sketchbook section.

## Novels

  
*see also [Hellboy Universe novels](/entry/Hellboy%20Universe%20novels)*  

In addition to the comics, there are novels that form part of the *Hellboy* series. Most of these are out of continuity except for the *Hellboy* novels written by author [Christopher Golden](/entry/Christopher%20Golden) (although his short stories are not).[3] The events of his first two books *The Lost Army* and *The Bones of Giants* are both featured in *The Hellboy Companion* official timeline, and characters and events have been on occasion referenced within the comics themselves.

## Other Media

Hellboy has also been adapted as a live-action film franchise (see *[Hellboy (film)](/entry/Hellboy%20%28film%29)* and *[Hellboy II: The Golden Army](/entry/Hellboy%20II%3A%20The%20Golden%20Army)*) and two direct-to-DVD animated films and its own spin-off comic book series (see *[Hellboy Animated](/entry/Hellboy%20Animated)*). It has also been made into a video game, *[Hellboy: The Science of Evil](/entry/Hellboy%3A%20The%20Science%20of%20Evil)*, and a role-playing-game.
