***Cool Your Head*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#5](/entry/Hellboy__Weird_Tales)*, both written and drawn by [Scott Morse](/entry/Scott%20Morse).

## Plot Synopsis

While on vacation, camping in Yosemite National Park [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28character%29) encounters an elderly man bathing in glacier melt. Although the two have a less than friendly conversation the old man is able to intuit that Hellboy is bothered by something and is attempting to clear his mind of what is burdening him with his venture into nature.
