**Krampus** was a magical entity and malevolent counterpart of Saint Nicholas. Wheras Saint Nicholas gave out gifts to good children, Krampus punished the bad ones using his stick and rides them around in a basket. However that interpretation is a more friendly version.

## History

Krampus posing as an old man Wilhelm Schulze in a small Europena village, caused a ruckus in the local church. The incident attracted the attention of Hellboy who was sent to investigate. Confronting 'Wilhelm' Hellboy was invited for supper. Hellboy questioned if he was really Krampus to which the old man confirmed. Seeing Hellboy skeptical, the old man tossed the paranormal investiagtor a skull of a child he killed in 1832. Through it Hellboy saw Krampus murder the boy.

Kramous threw away his human visage, asked Hellboy if he remembered Pandemonium. He proclaiming that he was a demon from Hell, exiled for a crime he had no knowledge of. He demanded to be returned to Hell, tired of his long existence on Earth. He attacked Hellboy goading the agent to kill him.

Hellboy tried t use his pistol, but the demon was resilent and demanded Hellboy use his hand. Try as he might Hellboy was at a disadvantage, until he somehow managed to stab the creature using a knife given to him by the ghosts of Krampus's victims.

Surprised but grateful Krampus readied to return to Hell. However he did not, and died, his corpse transformed into a goat.

Back at the Bureau Professor Trevor Bruttenholm explained that the topic Kramous had been debated by him and his colleague Harry Middleton. Middleton believed Krampus was actually the Demon Goat of the Wicthes; Sabbath, done up in a fancy dress for the holidays. Bruttenholm believed that it was just a nastier variation on the Scandinavian Yule Goat.

The Yule Goat or Joulupukki was the Pre-Christian Goat-man version of Father Christmas. Hellboy postulates that the Yule Goat forgot his origins along centuries and believed himself to be a Prince from Hell. When he got tired of killing kids he wanted to return to Hell. In the end Hellboy gave the magic goat a proper funeral, marked by a cross with toys laid out.[1]

## Abilities

Krampus possessed a number abilities, such as shape shifting and immortality. He was a highly durable creature that even bullets wouldn’t faze him. And he was imbued with enough strength to overpower Hellboy. Due to his extremely long life he had forgotten his origins, added on with his task of punishing children made him think he was a demon from Hell.
