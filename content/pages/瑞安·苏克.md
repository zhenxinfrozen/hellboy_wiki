**Ryan Sook** is an American comic book artist from San Jose, California. He has worked on *Arkham Asylum: Living Hell*, *The Spectre*, *Hawkman*, *Seven Soldiers: Zatanna*, *Buffy the Vampire Slayer* and *Angel*. He also illustrated *X-Factor*, written by Peter David at Marvel Comics. His style bears a strong resemblance to [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike%20Mignola) and Adam Hughes.
