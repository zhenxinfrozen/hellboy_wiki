***Shattered*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#5](/entry/Hellboy%3A%20Weird%20Tales)*, written by [Ron Marz](/entry/Ron%20Marz) with art provided by [Jim Starlin](/entry/Jim%20Starlin).

## Plot Synopsis

[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28character%29) is dispatched to Guatemala in 1979 by [Trevor Bruttenholm](/entry/Trevor%20Bruttenholm) to recover the Xul Chalak. In order to do so Hellboy has to wrestle if from the grips of a madman and a giant frog creature. Upon touching the Xul Chalak Hellboy sees a series of startling visions that disturb him. Deciding that the Xul Chalak is too powerful and likely to be abused he decides to break it before returning to [B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.) headquarters and handing over the pieces of the Xul Chalak to a disapointed Bruttenholm.
