***A Love Story*** is a non-canonical story published in *[Hellboy: Weird Tales \#7](/entry/Hellboy%3A%20Weird%20Tales)*, written and drawn by [Tommy Lee Edwards](/entry/Tommy%20Lee%20Edwards) with [Don Cameron](/entry/Don%20Cameron) assisting with the art.

## Plot Synopsis

In 1964 [Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28character%29) is tasked with returning a P-40 airplane from India to China following its restoration after being shot down in World War II. Upon its first successful flight following restoration it was discovered to be haunted. While flying the plane back to China Hellboy discovers the pilot when the plane was downed was a young man in love. Although the spirit makes the flight challenging Hellboy successfully returns the plane and in doing so reunites the spirit of the young pilot with the spirit of his young love and thus both spirits are put to rest.
