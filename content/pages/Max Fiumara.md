**Max Fiumara** is a comics artist. He made his Hellboy Universe debut in May 2012 with *B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth—The Transformation of J.H. O'Donnell*. He was one of two artists that worked on the [*Dark and Terrible* cycle](/entry/Dark%20and%20Terrible%20%28story%20cycle%29) of [*Abe Sapien*](/entry/Abe%20Sapien%20%28series%29).
