**Ben Stenbeck** is a New Zealand comics artist. He made his Hellboy Universe debut in June 2008 with [*B.P.R.D.: The Ectoplasmic Man*](/entry/The%20Ectoplasmic%20Man). He has worked extensively with [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike%20Mignola) on the *Baltimore* comics (not a part of the Hellboy Universe).
