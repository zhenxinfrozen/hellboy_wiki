***B.P.R.D.*** is a line of comic books about the [Bureau for Paranormal Research and Defense](/entry/Bureau%20for%20Paranormal%20Research%20and%20Defense) organisation. It is a spin-off of the *[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28series%29)* series.

## Publication History

The *B.P.R.D.* series made its [first appearance](/entry/B.P.R.D.%20%28teaser%29) as a serialised three-page teaser in *Dark Horse Extra* \#42-44 from December 2001 to February 2002 to advertise the upcoming miniseries *[Hollow Earth](/entry/Hollow%20Earth)*. Following the initial minseries, a series of one-shots by various writers and artists were produced which would fill the [second trade](/entry/TPB). The next miniseries, *[Plague of Frogs](/entry/Plague%20of%20Frogs%20%28story%29)* marked a turning point for the series. Mike Mignola took on writing duties for this story, and began to develop ongoing plotlines. Mike Mignola has maintained a writing credit on all subsequent *B.P.R.D.* stories. This minseries has often been cited by critics as the story that gave the series its direction. Appropriately, this minseries gave the [first story cycle](/entry/Plague%20of%20Frogs%20%28story%20cycle%29) its title. *Plague of Frogs* was also the story that established [Guy Davis](/entry/Guy%20Davis) as the ongoing artist.

Beginning with the short story *[Born Again](/entry/Born%20Again)*, [John Arcudi](/entry/John%20Arcudi) came aboard *B.P.R.D.* as the series' co-writer and he has done the bulk of the writing ever since, with Mike Mignola focusing more on the "big picture" stuff.

In 2010 the series began its second major story cycle, *[Hell on Earth](/entry/Hell_on_Earth_(story_cycle)))*, resulting in a logo change and an extended title. In 2011 Guy Davis retired as the ongoing artist, passing the baton onto [Tyler Crook](/entry/Tyler%20Crook). During the course of the cycle, Crook became one of multiple rotating artists including [James Harren](/entry/James%20Harren) and [Laurence Campbell](/entry/Laurence%20Campbell).

In late 2015 it was announced that John Arcudi was leaving the main B.P.R.D. title and that [Cometh the Hour](/entry/Cometh%20the%20Hour), the final story in the Hell on Earth saga, would be his last. However Arcudi continues to contribute to the [Lobster Johnson (series)](/entry/Lobster%20Johnson) series.

The third and final major story cycle, along with its creative team, has yet to be officially announced.

## Types of Stories

*B.P.R.D.* covers a wide range of stories. Unlike other *Hellboy* spin-offs, there is no central character.

### [*Plague of Frogs* cycle](/entry/Plague%20of%20Frogs%20%28story%20cycle%29)

  
*see also: List of **B.P.R.D.: Plague of Frogs** stories*

This story cycle was the primary ongoing plotline from 2002-2010. It was retroactively given the title *Plague of Frogs* when collected in [omnibus editions](/entry/B.P.R.D.%20Omnibus%20Editions). It primarily dealt with the B.P.R.D.'s conflict with the frog monsters, the Sub-terranian race, and [Memnan Saa](/entry/Memnan%20Saa).

### [*Hell on Earth* cycle](/entry/Hell_on_Earth_(story_cycle)))

  
*see also: List of **B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth** stories*

This is a direct continuation from the events of the *Plague of Frogs* cycle. It is the primary ongoing B.P.R.D. plotline from 2010 to present day. This cycle deals with the fallout out of [Liz](/entry/Elizabeth%20Sherman) torching the Hollow Earth in *[King of Fear](/entry/King%20of%20Fear%20%28story%29)* and the onset of the end of the world.

*Hell on Earth* has seen an increase in the volume of material released. In addition to the primary plotline which continues to come out on a monthly basis, there are side stories as well with a shift of focus on the regular B.P.R.D. agents.

### [*The Devil You Know* cycle](/entry/The%20Devil%20You%20Know%20%28story%20cycle%29)

  
*see also: List of **B.P.R.D.: The Devil You Know** stories*

This is a direct continuation from the events of the *Hell on Earth* cycle. It is scheduled to begin in the latter half of 2017.

### [*1946–1948*](/entry/1946%E2%80%931948%20%28story%20cycle%29)

  
*see also: List of **1946–1948** stories*

There are three miniseries that deal with the early years of the B.P.R.D., *[1946](/entry/1946)* (2008), *[1947](/entry/1947)* (2009) and *[1948](/entry/1948)* (2012). Originally this series was going to have a semi-regular release with roughly one miniseries coming out every year. "*We're hoping to do*B.P.R.D.: 1947*,*B.P.R.D.: 1948*,*B.P.R.D.: 1949*, and into the fifties*," said [Mignola](/entry/Mike%20Mignola) shortly before the release of *1946*.

This series was to be handled by [Joshua Dysart](/entry/Joshua%20Dysart) in much the same way the regular series is handled by [John Arcudi](/entry/John%20Arcudi). However, after *1947* Joshua Dysart left the series for unknown reasons and the final miniseries, *1948* has been written by Arcudi. Mike Mignola has said he felt he had too many series going on and needed to refocus his energies, which is likely the reason this series came to a premature close.

A form of these flashback stories were continued in 2015 with the release of a new series, *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.](/entry/Hellboy%20and%20the%20B.P.R.D.)* This series follows the early career of Hellboy, beginning with *[1952](/entry/1952)*. Each new trade collection explores a new year.

### [*Vampire*](/entry/Vampire%20%28story%20cycle%29)

  
*see also: List of **B.P.R.D.: Vampire** stories*

*Vampire* is a B.P.R.D. flashback story cycle, following on after the *1946–1948* cycle. It focuses on [Agent Simon Anders](/entry/Simon%20Anders) as he hunts down vampires. The series is written by Mike Mignola with Gabriel Bá and Fábio Moon. Bá and Moon also draw the comic. Currently only a single trade collection of *B.P.R.D.: Vampire* exists, although the number 1 on the trade collection suggests there will be more volumes in future.

### Others

Other stories do not fit neatly into any of the above categories. This handful of stories has been collected in the trade [*B.P.R.D.: Being Human*](/entry/TPB), an unnumbered *B.P.R.D.* volume.

## Numbering

Although *B.P.R.D.* has been released as a series of miniseries and one-shots, in addition to numbers within a given miniseries, it also has an ongoing numbering system on the inside cover for the series overall.

In 2012, coinciding with the release of the [100th issue of *B.P.R.D.*](/entry/The%20Return%20of%20the%20Master), the ongoing numbering would be listed on the front cover. This was because many readers seemed to think any *B.P.R.D.* story with a \#1 on it was a jumping on point. *B.P.R.D.* has always been a series of miniseries, but from issue 100 onwards, there was a concentrated effort to emphasise the ongoing nature of the story. Flashback stories, such as *[1948](/entry/1948)* and *[Vampire](/entry/Vampire%20%28story%29)* no longer carry the ongoing numbering.[1]

## Collections

### Trade Paperbacks

  
*see also [B.P.R.D. Trade Paperback Collections](/entry/TPB)*

*see also [B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth Trade Paperback Collections](/entry/TPB)*

*see also [B.P.R.D.: The Devil You Know Trade Paperback Collections](/entry/TPB)*

*see also [B.P.R.D.: Vampire Trade Paperback Collections](/entry/TPB)*

All in-continuity *B.P.R.D.* stories are collected into trade paperbacks eventually. So far there has been sixteen volumes, plus an additional eleven for *B.P.R.D.: Hell on Earth,* and another for *Vampire*. The standard trades usually contain a sketchbook or gallery and most stories come with an afterword by one of the authors.

*1946*<span> and </span>*1947*<span> were originally numbered volumes 9 and 13 of the original </span>*B.P.R.D.*<span> trade collections, but since the printing of the </span>[*Plague of Frogs* omnibuses](/entry/B.P.R.D.%20Omnibus%20Editions)<span> all reprints have been unnumbered. </span>*1948,*<span> which was first printed after the *Plague of Frogs* omnibuses, has never been numbered.</span>

### Omnibus Editions

  
*see also [B.P.R.D. Omnibus Editions](/entry/B.P.R.D.%20Omnibus%20Editions)*

So far the [*Plague of Frogs* cycle](/entry/Plague%20of%20Frogs%20%28story%20cycle%29), *[1946–1948](/entry/1946%E2%80%931948%20%28story%20cycle%29)*, and *[*Hell on Earth* cycle](/entry/Hell_on_Earth_(story_cycle))) have been collected in omnibus editions. Scott Allie has stated that*[The Devil You Know](/entry/The%20Devil%20You%20Know%20%28story%20cycle%29)*will also be collected in the format when the series is finished.*
