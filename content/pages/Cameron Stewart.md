**Cameron Stewart** is a comics writer and artist. He made his Hellboy Universe debut in August 2004 with [*B.P.R.D.: Another Day at the Office*](/entry/Another%20Day%20at%20the%20Office). The character [Ashley Strode](/entry/Ashley%20Strode) was set aside specifically for Stewart to work with, until she was required for the *[Devil You Know](/entry/The%20Devil%20You%20Know%20%28story%20cycle%29)* cycle of *[B.P.R.D.](/entry/B.P.R.D.%20%28series%29)*
