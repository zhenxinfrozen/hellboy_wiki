***Witchfinder***is a line of comic books about the character [Sir Edward Grey](/entry/Sir%20Edward%20Grey). It is a spin-off of the*[Hellboy](/entry/Hellboy%20%28series%29)'' series. The full series title (though rarely used) is***Sir Edward Grey, Witchfinder**''.

## Publication History

Sir Edward Grey was first mentioned in *[Hellboy: Wake the Devil](/entry/Wake%20the%20Devil)* and since then has had a number of relatively minor appearances in various *Hellboy* comics, sometimes appearing as a cloaked figure wearing a mask. Grey was featured prominently in the first issue of *[Abe Sapien: The Drowning](/entry/The%20Drowning)*.

Later that same year, the *Witchfinder* series made its debut with the short story *[Murderous Intent](/entry/Murderous%20Intent)* in *MySpace Dark Horse Presents* \#16. This short story was designed to introduce Sir Edward Grey in advance of the miniseries *[In the Service of Angels](/entry/In%20the%20Service%20of%20Angels)*. Both stories were written by [Mike Mignola](/entry/Mike%20Mignola) with [Ben Stenbeck](/entry/Ben%20Stenbeck) on art.

The second miniseries, *[Lost and Gone Forever](/entry/Lost%20and%20Gone%20Forever)*, came out in 2011, this time with [John Arcudi](/entry/John%20Arcudi) handling writing duties and [John Severin](/entry/John%20Severin) on art. This was Severin's last complete comic before he died.

The third miniseries, *[The Mysteries of Unland](/entry/The%20Mysteries%20of%20Unland)*, was published in 2014. This story is unique among the *Witchfinder* titles in that it does not give Mike Mignola a writing credit. *The Mysteries of Unland* was written by [Kim Newman](/entry/Kim%20Newman) and [Maura McHugh](/entry/Maura%20McHugh) with [Tyler Crook](/entry/Tyler%20Crook) on art duties. The final pages of this story tie into a flashback sequence in *[Abe Sapien: The Shadow Over Suwanee](/entry/The%20Shadow%20Over%20Suwanee)*, also drawn by Crook.

*[Beware the Ape](/entry/Beware%20the%20Ape)*, a short story set in between *Lost and Gone Forever* and *The Mysteries of Unland*, came in May 2014 in *Dark Horse Presents* \#36. This was written by Mike Mignola with art by Ben Stenbeck. Though only a short story, it ties in with the larger mythology of a recurring Cthulhu-like statues (seen in *[Lobster Johnson: Tony Masso's Finest Hour](/entry/Tony%20Masso%27s%20Finest%20Hour)* and several *[Hellboy and the B.P.R.D.](/entry/Hellboy%20and%20the%20B.P.R.D.)* stories).

In 2016, [Chris Roberson](/entry/Chris%20Roberson) became the ongoing series writer for *Witchfinder* beginning with *[City of the Dead](/entry/City%20of%20the%20Dead)* (with Ben Stenbeck on art duties). Mike Mignola was so impressed with Roberson's work on the title, he was invited to write several other titles as well.

## Collections

### Trade Paperbacks

  
*see also [Witchfinder Trade Paperback Collections](/entry/TPB)*  
 

All in-continuity *Witchfinder* stories are collected into trade paperbacks eventually. So far there has been four volumes. The standard trades usually contain a sketchbook or gallery with an afterword by the author.
